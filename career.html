Marketing Executive/ Co-ordinate

Marketing Executive – Industrial Marketing & Content Creation

Location: Navi Mumbai (Preferred)
Company: Furnisys

About the Role

We are looking for a proactive Marketing Executive to support our branding, content, digital marketing, and sales enablement initiatives. The ideal candidate should be organized, creative, and eager to learn industrial marketing while working closely with sales, channel partners, and product teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create and coordinate marketing collaterals, brochures, flyers, presentations, and social media content.

  • Manage website content updates and marketing campaigns.

  • Assist in content writing for blogs, case studies, product pages, and email campaigns.

  • Support channel partner marketing initiatives and lead generation activities.

  • Coordinate with designers, agencies, and vendors for marketing deliverables.

  • Maintain marketing databases, lead records, and campaign reports.

  • Assist in event, exhibition, and webinar planning.

  • Conduct market and competitor research.

Preferred Skills

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.

  • Basic understanding of digital marketing and social media.

  • Experience with Canva, Zoho automations, Zoho tools (preferred)

  • Familiarity with LinkedIn marketing and content creation.

  • Good organizational and project coordination skills.

  • Knowledge of B2B or industrial marketing is an added advantage.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field.

  • 0–2 years of experience in marketing, content, or digital marketing roles.

What You’ll Gain

  • Exposure to industrial and B2B marketing.

  • Hands-on experience in branding, content strategy, digital marketing, and sales enablement.

  • Opportunity to work directly on high-impact projects and grow into a larger marketing role.

📩 Interested candidates can send their resume to marketing@furnisys.co
