Workbench for Material Testing

Why Material Testing Needs a Strong Foundation

In Oil & Gas R&D labs, material testing is not just another routine procedure, it is a vital step that decides whether components can survive extreme conditions such as high pressure, elevated temperatures, and corrosive environments.

At the centre of this process is the Universal Testing Machine (UTM). This machine measures tensile, compressive, and elongation properties of metals, elastomers, and other critical materials. The results determine whether downhole tools, sealing systems, and structural parts are fit for real-world use.

To perform reliably, a UTM requires more than just precision engineering, it needs a Workbench for Material Testing that provides safety, strength, and long-term stability.

The Challenge: Supporting High-Value Equipment

Placing a UTM on a weak or unstable surface can lead to vibrations, shocks, or even toppling risks. These issues threaten not only the accuracy of test results but also the integrity of high-value equipment.

The requirement was simple but crucial:

A durable, load-bearing workbench to support the UTM.

A setup that reduces risks of vibrations and unintended movement.

A configuration designed for safety, reliability, and day-to-day efficiency.

Furnisys Solution:

Dual Workbench Setup

To meet these requirements, Furnisys developed a dual workbench configuration tailored specifically for UTM-based material testing. The solution focused on both stability and workflow efficiency.

Front Workbench
Storage-Integrated Strength

Designed with two base cabinets for organized storage.

A stainless steel worktop ensured a durable, heavy-duty surface.

Positioned at the front for easy operator access during testing.

Provided storage for fixtures, tools, and calibration accessories, keeping the lab organized.

Rear Workbench
Stable Mounting Base for UTM

Built without underbench storage for maximum structural strength.

Offered a vibration-free surface dedicated solely to mounting the UTM.

Strong construction ensured stability during high-force tensile and compression tests.

Open-frame design improved operator access and reduced handling risks.

Benefits of the Dual Workbench Setup

Safety First

Protects high-value UTM equipment from instability and accidental impacts.

Improved Accuracy

Eliminates vibrations that could compromise test results.

Organized Workflow

Front bench storage keeps accessories and fixtures within easy reach.

Operator Confidence

Clear access and stable mounting reduce chances of errors.

Long-Term Reliability

A foundation built to last, supporting years of precise testing.

Conclusion: A Workbench Built for Material Testing

By combining two specialized benches into a single solution, Furnisys created a safer and more controlled environment for UTM-based testing. The Workbench for Material Testing not only safeguarded precision instruments but also improved efficiency and operator confidence.

For industries like Oil & Gas, where accuracy and reliability directly impact field performance, this dual workbench solution proved to be an essential investment.
