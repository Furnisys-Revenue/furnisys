Flexi Single-Sided Adder Unit

"Designed to connect with any single-sided Flexi starter or corner unit" to extend worksurface length and accessory support.

Get a quoteDownload catalogue

How it fits your work.

Need more workspace? The Single-Sided Adder lets you extend your existing single-sided Flexi Workstation Starter Unit seamlessly. Simply attach the adder to the starter or corner module to create a larger, continuous work area tailored to your operations.

Width24″, 30″, 36″, 48″, 60″, 72″
Depth24″, 28″, 30″, 34″
Height54″, 72″, 84″
IncludesAdder frame assembly; Footrest for ergonomic comfort; 2 outriggers for stability; Worksurface assembly (available in PLW, PELW, HPL, or SS Cladded worktop); Single-sided power beam (15 Amp) with integrated light switch; Light fixture assembly for task illumination
