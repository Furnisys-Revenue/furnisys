Stop operators searching for tools at the assembly station

Give every tool a fixed place within arm's reach of the operator.

Industrial SystemWall System
Request this setupAll solutions

The problem

Operators leave the bench, borrow tools from the next station or dig through a drawer to find the right one. Each search is short, but across a shift and a line it adds up to lost output.

Why it happens

Tools have no fixed home. They sit on the worktop, in a shared drawer or on a side table, so nobody can see at a glance what is missing or where it went.

How the setup comes together

  1. List the tools used at the station and how often each one is used.
  2. Put daily tools on the uprighter pegboard at eye level, in the order they are used.
  3. Move weekly tools to the mobile cabinet drawers and shared tools to the Wall System panel.
  4. Mark each position so a missing tool is visible immediately.

What changes

Tools are in sight and in reach, missing items are spotted at a glance, and operators stay at the bench.

The setup

  • Base Series workbenchStable worksurface sized to the job
  • Link uprighter, shelves and overhead cabinetsUprighter with pegboard and bin rails above the worktop
  • Wall System pegboardShadow-marked tool positions on the wall behind the line
  • Mobile cabinetDrawers for spares and less-used tools under or beside the bench

All standard modules. Sizes, worktops and colours are chosen for your space.
