A Mobile Tool Trolley for an Army Engine Maintenance Workshop

A Link Mobile Trolley with pegboards, tool holders and an adjustable shelf, configured to move between twelve zones of a newly built engine workshop.

A new workshop with twelve working zones

Situation

A Central Government (Army) workshop was setting up a new engine maintenance area. The layout they shared had twelve zones: old spares, engine disassembly ramp, SMT and tool racks, cleaning and weighing, engine assembly ramp, injector test bench, final inspection, load test, rotable bay, instrument cluster repair, a canopy area and the air compressor.

Tools needed to travel with the technicians

The challenge

Technicians move constantly between disassembly, testing and repair. Fixed tool storage in one corner would mean repeated walking and waiting. The workshop needed a trolley that would carry the right tools to each zone and keep them organised on the way.

How do you put the tool store on wheels?

The question

How do you configure one trolley that serves engine disassembly, injector testing and instrument cluster repair equally well, and still has room for parts in transit?

From first proposal to final configuration

The Furnisys approach

Initial configuration

A Link Mobile Trolley (60" H × 48" W) on heavy-duty castors with two pegboards (18" and 30") and accessories for the main tool families.

Final configuration

When the client asked for an adjustable shelf, we reworked the layout to two 18" pegboards plus the shelf, on the same trolley frame. Accessories:

  • Screwdriver holder for fine adjustments in instrument cluster repair and canopy tasks
  • Bin strip for fasteners, nuts and bolts used in engine assembly and fitting
  • Medium slotted hooks for wrenches and frequently used hand tools
  • Spanner tray for engine repair, calibration and assembly
  • Allen key holder for precise adjustments on engine components
  • Adjustable shelf for tools, spares and parts awaiting inspection

One trolley across the whole workshop

How the setup works together

The trolley moves between the disassembly and assembly ramps, sits beside the injector test bench and final inspection area, and carries a selected tool set to the rotable bay and cluster repair. The shelf holds parts temporarily in the old spares, cleaning and rotable areas before they are inspected or reused.

What the workshop received

What the configuration enabled

  • Tools organised and visible at every work zone
  • Less walking between fixed storage and the job
  • Space for spares and components in transit
  • A configuration refined with the client before delivery

Mobility is a layout decision

Furnisys perspective

In a workshop with many zones, a mobile tool station can do more than a row of fixed cabinets. The layout told us where the trolley would travel, and that shaped the accessories.

Frequently asked questions

What size is the Link Mobile Trolley used here?

60 inches high and 48 inches wide, on heavy-duty castors, with two 18-inch pegboards and an adjustable shelf.

Which accessories suit an engine maintenance trolley?

Screwdriver holder, bin strip, medium slotted hooks, spanner tray and Allen key holder, plus a shelf for spares and parts in transit.

Can the configuration change after the first proposal?

Yes. In this project the pegboard sizes were changed to make room for an adjustable shelf the client asked for, using the same frame.
