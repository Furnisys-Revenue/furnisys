# Weatherford

Optimizing R&D Efficiency in Oil Equipment and Services: How Furnisys Transforms Weatherford's Testing Labs

R&D is central to oil equipment and services companies like Weatherford (drilling equipment, evaluation technologies, production solutions); success depends partly on organised, ergonomic labs; Furnisys supplied specialised furniture and systems for Weatherford's R&D facilities.

Complication: The Need for Specialized, Durable Furniture System and Layout

  • Slicing machines, grinders, ovens and furnaces for metal and rubber testing.
  • Universal Testing Machines (UTMs), climate chambers and heat aging ovens for testing PCBs and other electronic components.
  • Adequate storage, ESD protection and ergonomic features.

Layout had to support material preparation through mechanical and electrical testing, with fume hoods and markerboards for SOPs.

Question: How Can Furnisys Optimize the R&D Environment?

Tailor-made approach matching products to each R&D area.

Answer: Furnisys' Configurable Solutions for Weatherford's R&D Lab

1. Preparation Area (Shed Area): Streamlining Material Processing — Pro Workbench with SS worktop and Base Cabinets for ovens and grinders; Pro Workbench rated up to 1000 kg allowed two ovens to be mounted; SS worktop durable and easy to clean.

2. Rubber and Metal Testing Area: Ensuring Precise Material Analysis — Pro Workbenches with HPL and SS worktops (heat, chemical, wear resistant) supporting FTIR Spectrometers and Differential Scanning Calorimeters; standard worktops could deteriorate causing contamination, inaccurate results, safety risks, higher maintenance. Markerboards and tackboards for SOPs; Flexi Workstations and planned storage.

3. PCB Testing Area: Ensuring Reliability of Electronic Components — ESD-safe workstations; static too weak to feel can still degrade PCB components; without ESD precautions: failure, rework, delays, reputational damage. Workbenches with PLW worktops and Base Cabinets supported a UTM; climate control chamber and heat aging oven simulated environmental stress.

Additional Accessories: Pegboards, screwdriver holders, privacy panels.

Additional Custom Features:

Footrests on the FTIR Spectrometer workbench; heavy-gauge footrests and rear support channels on the UTM Preparation Table.

Conclusion: A Seamless Integration of Efficiency and Precision

Lab became more organised, efficient and ergonomic, supporting high-quality oil & gas research and testing.
