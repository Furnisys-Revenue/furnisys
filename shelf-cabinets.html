Shelf Storage Cabinets

An efficient solution to store, organize, and access bulky equipment or irregularly shaped items that aren’t ideal for drawer storage.

Key features and benefits.

Lockable Doors

Available in both single and double-door variants to ensure safe and secure access to stored items.

Smooth Sliding Doors

Sliding doors offer convenient access while minimizing footprint—ideal for narrow or tight spaces.

Full-Access Hinged Doors

Hinged door options open fully, allowing maximum visibility and unrestricted access to stored contents
