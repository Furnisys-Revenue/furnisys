R&D & Design Studio

The design studio is where jewellery concepts move from sketching and CAD/CAM modelling to wax prototyping and sample validation. The workflow constantly shift between digital design, hand-finishing and small-batch sampling.

Workspace challenges
  • Changing Work ModesDesigners move frequently between digital and hand-work activities.
  • Workspace ClutterSamples, tools and reference materials can quickly occupy valuable work surface.
  • Limited FlexibilityFixed furniture forces designers to adapt their workflow around the workstation.
  • Sample OrganisationPrototypes and reference designs need accessible, organised storage.

Melting, Casting & Powder Handling

Melting and casting transform gold, silver and alloy stock into ingots or rough forms before shaping. This is a high-heat, high-value environment where molten metal and powder must be handled with controlled containment and minimal loss.

Workspace challenges
  • Heat & ImpactSurfaces must with stand demanding high-temperature handling.
  • Fumes & ParticulatesThe workspace requires appropriate containment and ventilation.
  • Material LossPowder and scrap require controlled recovery.
  • Material AccountabilityPrecious-metal movement must remain traceable.
What we can provide
  • Heat- and chemical-resistant worktops
  • GI, SS cladding and HPL worktop
  • Ventilated workstation zones
  • Segregated powder and scrap storage
Worktops
Operational outcome
  • Safer working environment
  • Better process containment
  • Improved fume control
  • More reliable precious-metal recovery

Wire Drawing & Metal Forming

Wire drawing reduces cast metal into fine wire through progressive dies, feeding chain-making and other downstream processes. The work is repetitive and physically demanding, requiring stable work surfaces and consistent die alignment.

Workspace challenges
  • VibrationUnstable benches can affect draw consistency.
  • Operator StrainRepetitive work demands ergonomic support.
  • Die ChangeoversPoorly organised die storage slows process changes.
  • Load HandlingWork surfaces must withstand metal-forming loads.
What we can provide
  • Stable, load-rated workstations
  • GI-cladded worktops
  • Integrated die and tool storage
  • Footrests
  • Mobile die storage
Worktops
Operational outcome

Tool Room & Die Mould Making

Diamond Cutting & Stone Setting

Diamond cutting, faceting and stone-setting require exceptional surface stability and focused lighting. Even minor movement can affect precision and increase the risk of losing small, high-value stones.

Workspace challenges
  • Surface StabilityPrecision cutting requires a rigid work platform.
  • Task LightingDetailed work requires focused illumination.
  • Stone SecuritySmall stones must remain contained and controlled.
  • Material LossA misplaced gemstone represents direct material loss.

  • Focused task lighting

Hallmarking & Purity Certification

Hallmarking is a critical compliance check point where precious-metal purity is verified and stamped. Accuracy, sample control and documentation are as important as the physical certification process.

Workspace challenges
  • Sample SegregationMixed samples create a risk of identification errors.
  • Controlled SurfacesDefined work zones help prevent material mix-ups.
  • Secure StoragePending and completed stock requires controlled storage.
  • DocumentationRecords must remain organised and traceable.
What we can provide
  • Stable workstations
  • Segregated sample storage
  • Defined surface zoning
  • Documentation storage
Operational outcome
  • Controlled certification workspace
  • Reduced sample mix-ups
  • Secure material handling
  • Audit-ready documentation

Plating & Surface Finishing

Plating and surface-treatment processes use chemical baths and solutions to apply finish coatings. These wet processes require containment, ventilation and physical separation from dry precision work.

Workspace challenges
  • Chemical ExposureOrdinary surfaces can deteriorate quickly.
  • Wet Process ContainmentPlating operations require controlled work areas.
  • VentilationChemical handling requires appropriate ventilation.
  • Cross-ContaminationWet processes must remain separated from dry finishing areas.
What we can provide
  • Wooden worktop with SS cladding, HPL worktop
  • Segregated storage for plating solutions and consumables
  • Ventilated shelving

Tumbling, Polishing & Laser Marking

Tumbling and polishing refine surface finishes, while laser marking adds identification and branding details. Both are late-stage processes where surface quality and precision are critical.

Workspace challenges
  • Dust & DebrisFine debris from polishing can affect adjacent precision work.
  • VibrationTumbling equipment can introduce vibration into the workspace.
  • Process ZoningLaser marking benefits from separation from dusty processes.
  • Precision FinishingOperators need stable, organised workspaces for detailed finishing.

Frequently asked questions.

Can one system cover every jewellery manufacturing space?

Yes. Work systems, storage systems and accessories share standard modules, so each space gets its own configuration within one consistent system.

Can the setup change as our operations grow?

Yes. Stations are built from standard parts, so you can add drawers, power, uprighters or bays, or relocate stations, without replacing what you already have.

Do you plan the layout before manufacturing?

For confirmed projects we carry out a site study and prepare 2D and 3D layouts and a proposal before anything is manufactured.
