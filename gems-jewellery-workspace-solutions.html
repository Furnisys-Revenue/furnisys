GEMS & JEWELLERY

Gems & Jewellery Manufacturing Workspace Solutions

Purpose-built workspace systems for precision, security, and material accountability across the jewellery production floor.

Gems and jewellery manufacturing facilities bring together fine craftsmanship and industrial process control within the same footprint. Furnisys approaches each space as a functional system, combining workstations, storage, organisation and material handling around the application.

EXPLORE SPACES TALK TO AN EXPERT

WHY GEMS & JEWELLERY SPACES REQUIRE AN APPLICATION-SPECIFIC APPROACH

Application-Specific Workspaces Designed for Jewellery Manufacturing

Each stage, from melting to hallmarking, has different heat, security, and precision requirements. The workspace needs to support the process taking place inside it.

PRECISION

Vibration-resistant, well-lit surfaces for cutting, setting, and certification.

SECURITY

Lockable, segregated storage for precious metal and in-process stones.

TRACEABILITY

Zoned surfaces and documentation storage that support BIS/ISO compliance.

DURABILITY

Heat-, chemical-, and impact-resistant worktops for melting, forming, and plating.
