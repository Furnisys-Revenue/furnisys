R&D & Design Studio

The design studio is where new jewellery pieces are conceived — from sketching and CAD/CAM modelling to wax prototyping and sample validation before a design moves to production. Work here shifts constantly between digital design, hand-finishing, and small-batch sampling.

ISO 9001 quality management expectations apply to design-to-production handoff and sample traceability. Where 3D printing or wax-based prototyping equipment is used, ventilation and fire-safety provisions under the Factories Act, 1948 also apply.
The challenge

Fixed furniture forces designers to work around the bench rather than the other way round. Frequent switching between CAD stations, wax carving, and sample review slows iteration and clutters a space that should support fast, creative turnaround.

What we provide

We provide modular, reconfigurable design workstations with mixed digital and hand-work surfaces, task lighting, and mobile storage for samples and reference designs, so the studio can adapt as design workflows change.

ScitecFlexiOverhead cabinetMobile cabinet

Outcome: A flexible design workspace that supports faster prototyping, protects sample integrity, and keeps design-to-production handoff organised.

Melting, Casting & Powder Handling

This is where gold, silver, and alloy stock are melted and cast into ingots or rough forms before shaping. It is a high-heat, high-value zone where precious metal in molten and powder form must be handled without loss or contamination.

Factories Act, 1948 fire and ventilation safety provisions and Pollution Control Board (CPCB/SPCB) norms for fume and effluent handling apply. BIS practices for precious metal accountability support material traceability from this stage onward.
The challenge

Heat, fumes, and fine metal particulate make this one of the highest-risk zones on the floor. Without proper containment and heat-resistant surfaces, both operator safety and precious-metal recovery suffer, and material loss is difficult to trace.

What we provide

We provide heat- and chemical-resistant worktops, GI-cladded surfaces for high-impact and high-heat handling, ventilated workstation zones, and segregated storage for powder and scrap recovery to minimise material loss.

ProSpecialty workbenchBase cabinetMobile cabinet

Outcome: A safer, better-contained melting and casting area with improved fume control and more reliable precious-metal recovery.

Wire Drawing & Metal Forming

Wire drawing reduces cast metal into fine wire and forms through progressive dies, feeding chain-making and other downstream processes. The work is repetitive, physically demanding, and depends on consistent die alignment.

ISO 9001 process-consistency expectations apply, along with general Factories Act, 1948 ergonomic and machine-guarding safety requirements for die-drawing equipment.
The challenge

An unstable or vibrating bench affects draw consistency and increases operator strain over long runs. Poorly organised die storage also slows changeovers between wire gauges and profiles.

What we provide

We provide stable, load-rated workstations with GI-cladded worktops suited to metal-forming loads, integrated die and tool storage, footrests for ergonomic comfort, and mobile cabinets for quick die changeovers.

ProBase cabinetMobile cabinetOverhead cabinet

Outcome: A stable, ergonomic wire-drawing station that supports consistent draw quality and faster die changeovers.

Tool Room & Die / Mould Making

The tool room fabricates and maintains the dies, moulds, and jigs used across production — precision work that keeps every downstream process running to spec.

ISO 9001 tool-control and calibration-adjacent practices apply, alongside general workshop safety requirements under the Factories Act, 1948 for grinding and machining equipment.
The challenge

Tool rooms handle a very wide mix of small precision tooling. Without organised, accessible storage, tools go missing or mismatched dies get pulled, causing rework and downtime elsewhere on the floor.

What we provide

We provide Pro leg workstations with base cabinets for integrated tool storage, pegboards for frequently used tools, variable-angle shelving for small components, and mobile cabinets for point-of-use tool access.

ProBase cabinetDrawer cabinetPegboardOverhead cabinetMobile cabinet

Outcome: An organised tool room where dies and tooling are easy to locate, reducing downstream rework and changeover delays.

Diamond Cutting & Stone Setting

Diamond cutting, faceting, and stone-setting stations require absolute surface stability and excellent lighting, since even minor bench movement affects cut precision and gemstone security.

ISO 9001 process-control expectations apply to cutting consistency. Where applicable, GIA/gemological grading protocols guide inspection practices, and Factories Act, 1948 lighting standards apply to precision workstations.
The challenge

Vibration, poor task lighting, and unsecured work surfaces put both cut quality and small, high-value stones at risk. A dropped or misplaced stone at this stage is a direct material loss.

What we provide

We provide rigid, vibration-resistant precision workstations with postform laminated worktops, focused task lighting, tray-and-bin systems for stone containment, and lockable storage for in-process gemstones.

ScitecOverhead cabinetSpecialty storageMobile cabinet

Outcome: A stable, well-lit cutting and setting station that protects cut precision and reduces the risk of stone loss.

Chain Making & Assembly

Chain-making areas link, solder, and assemble fine components into finished chains — high-volume, repetitive work where space efficiency matters as much as precision.

ISO 9001 workmanship consistency expectations apply, along with Factories Act, 1948 provisions for soldering fume extraction and workstation safety.
The challenge

Chain-making stations are often the most space-constrained on the floor. Bulky furniture obstructs workflow and slows access to soldering equipment and fine tools positioned close to the operator.

What we provide

We provide compact Scitec workstations with hanging drawers for lightweight tools, rear-mounted pegboards, instrument riser shelving with power support, and footrests suited to extended seated work.

ScitecPegboardOverhead cabinetMobile cabinetFlexi

Outcome: A space-efficient chain-making station that keeps tools within reach without crowding the work area.

Hallmarking & Purity Certification (Bismarking)

Hallmarking stations verify and stamp precious metal purity — a compliance checkpoint where accuracy and documentation matter as much as the physical stamping process itself.

BIS Hallmarking (IS 1417 and the BIS Hallmarking Scheme, including HUID) governs this process directly. ISO/IEC 17025 practices apply where in-house assaying or testing is performed.
The challenge

Any ambiguity in this zone — mixed samples, uncontrolled surfaces, poor record-keeping — creates direct compliance exposure, since hallmarking accuracy is legally regulated, not just a quality preference.

What we provide

We provide dedicated, stable workstations with segregated sample storage, clear surface zoning to prevent mix-ups, and secure cabinet storage for stamped and pending stock.

ScitecDrawer cabinetSpecialty storageOverhead cabinet

Outcome: A controlled hallmarking station that supports accurate, auditable purity certification in line with BIS requirements.

Tumbling, Polishing & Laser Marking

Tumbling and polishing refine surface finish after forming, while laser marking adds fine identification and branding detail — both are late-stage, detail-critical processes before final inspection.

ISO 9001 finishing-consistency expectations apply. Where laser equipment is used, general machine and laser-safety provisions under the Factories Act, 1948 also apply.
The challenge

Dust, fine debris, and vibration from tumbling equipment can affect the precision needed for laser marking if the two processes share poorly zoned space.

What we provide

We provide postform laminated worktops suited to polishing and finishing tasks, rear-mounted pegboards, instrument riser shelving for laser equipment, and footrests for extended finishing work.

ScitecPegboardOverhead cabinetFlexiMobile cabinet

Outcome: A clean, well-organised finishing and marking area that supports consistent surface quality and accurate detailing.

Weighing, Quality Inspection & Secure Tool Crib

This zone brings together precision weighing, final quality inspection, and secure storage of tools, consumables, and in-process precious metal — the last checkpoint before stock moves on or off the floor.

The Legal Metrology Act governs certified weighing instruments. ISO 10012 supports calibration control, and DGCA-style disciplined tool-control principles (adapted from AS9110-type practices) support accountability for tooling and stock movement.
The challenge

Mixed storage of calibrated weighing instruments with general tools invites measurement error and accountability gaps — a serious concern when every gram of precious metal is tracked.

What we provide

We provide stable precision workstations for weighing and inspection, high-density secure storage for tools and consumables, compatible base cabinets rated up to 65 kg UDL, and mobile cabinets for point-of-use access.

Outcome: A controlled, accountable checkpoint that supports accurate weighing, audit-readiness, and secure material handling.

Frequently asked questions.

Which standards do jewellery manufacturing workspaces need to support?

Depending on the space, requirements commonly include BIS Hallmarking (IS 1417, IEC 17025 practices apply where in-house assaying or testing is performed., ISO 9001 finishing-consistency expectations apply. Where laser equipment is used, ISO 9001 process-consistency expectations apply, ISO 9001 process-control expectations apply to cutting consistency. Where applicable, ISO 9001 quality management expectations apply to design-to-production handoff. We configure worktops, grounding, storage and segregation to support them.

Can one system cover every jewellery manufacturing space?

Yes. Work systems, storage systems and accessories share standard modules, so each space gets its own configuration within one consistent system.

Can the setup change as our operations grow?

Yes. Stations are built from standard parts, so you can add drawers, power, uprighters or bays, or relocate stations, without replacing what you already have.

Do you plan the layout before manufacturing?

For confirmed projects we carry out a site study and prepare 2D and 3D layouts and a proposal before anything is manufactured.
