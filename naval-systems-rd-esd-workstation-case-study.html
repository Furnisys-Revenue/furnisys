A Heavy-Duty ESD Workstation for Naval Systems R&D

A Base Workstation rated for 360–454 kg with ESD mat, wrist strap and grounding block, and a Link Uprighter with pegboard, power rail and shelf, configured for naval electro-mechanical R&D.

Shipboard machinery and test systems

Situation

The client designs, manufactures, overhauls and supports shipboard machinery, critical mechanical systems and test facilities for submarines and surface ships, and also serves clean energy applications. Their R&D facility develops and validates electro-mechanical assemblies, control modules and instrumentation.

Heavy parts and sensitive electronics on one bench

The challenge

The workstation had to carry heavy components and test fixtures while protecting sensitive monitoring, control and diagnostic electronics from static. It also needed integrated power for instruments and tools, and organised storage for iterative R&D work.

Can a heavy-duty bench also be ESD-safe?

The question

How do you combine defence-grade strength with static control, so one bench can hold a heavy fixture and a control board at the same time?

A Base Workstation with ESD and uprighter accessories

The Furnisys approach

  • Base Workstation rated for a UDL of 360–454 kg, with a durable postform laminated worktop
  • ESD accessories: ESD mat, wrist strap and grounding block
  • Link Uprighter with pegboard, bin strip, screwdriver holder and medium slotted hooks
  • Horizontal power rail with two switched sockets and indicator lamps
  • Adjustable shelf for instruments, documentation and small test rigs

Heavy work below, controlled electronics above

How the setup works together

Fixtures and subassemblies sit on the worktop, which is grounded through the ESD mat and block. Instruments are powered from the rail and stand on the shelf, while tools and fasteners stay visible on the pegboard.

What the facility received

What the configuration enabled

  • A sturdy bench for heavy naval components
  • Controlled static dissipation for electronics
  • Safe, accessible power without loose cables
  • Organised storage that supports 5S in R&D

Strength and safety together

Furnisys perspective

ESD protection is an accessory decision, not a separate bench. That lets heavy-duty frames serve electronics work too. See our government and defence solutions.

Frequently asked questions

What load can a Base Workstation carry?

This configuration was rated for a uniformly distributed load of 360–454 kg.

How is a heavy-duty bench made ESD-safe?

With an ESD mat on the worktop, a wrist strap and a grounding block to dissipate static safely.

What power options are available on the uprighter?

Here a horizontal power rail with two switched sockets and indicator lamps was fitted.
