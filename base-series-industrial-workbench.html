Base Series Workbench

All purpose workbench series designed to functionally serve your basic workspace needs.

Base Workbench Worksurface Options

Base workbenches are available in twelve standard sizes and two heights. Simply choose the base size, height and worksurface that best suits your needs, and add appropriate accessories.

Base Workbench

  • Workbench height is 30” (76 cm) or 36” (91 cm)
  • Heavy-duty 2” x 2” tubular legs include levelling glides
  • Load capacity for bench is 1,000 lbs. (454 kg) evenly distributed

Worksurface Material Legend

  • PLW = Postform Laminated Particle Board
  • PELW = Postform Electrostatic Laminated Particle Board
  • GW = GI + Plyboard
  • SSW = SS + Plyboard
  • HPL = High Pressure Laminate

Base All Purpose Workbench

“L” Conﬁguration

Available in multiple sizes and with 2 piece top or 3 piece top with diagonal corner piece. Two piece tops are all square edge. Three piece tops in laminate and plyboard have front radius edge. Both may be assembled with left hand or right hand orientation. This conﬁguration eliminates the knee space interference of the bench leg that occurs when two independent benches are aligned in the “L” conﬁguration.

Base Workbench Leg Extenders

Leg Extenders

  • Allow nominal height adjustment of 6” on 2” increments
  • Extender for 30” high workbench allows extended heights of 32”, 34” or 36” overall height
  • Extender for 36” high workbench allows extended heights of 38”, 40” or 42” overall height.

Base Workbench Accessories

Back And End Stops

  • Stops extend approx. 4” above workbench top

Standard Casters

  • Includes 2 swivel and 2 locking swivel casters

Outlet Strip a

Base Series Industrial Workbenches – Functional Simplicity, Industrial Strength

Furnisys' Base Series Industrial Workbench is a purpose-built solution engineered for performance across workshops, labs, warehouses, and production floors. Designed as a foundational platform for daily industrial tasks, this modular workbench system combines flexibility, durability, and user comfort—all essential for modern manufacturing environments.

Whether you need a workshop workbench for repairs, a warehouse workbench for material handling, or a factory workbench for daily production, the Base Series adapts with ease. It serves as an ideal production workbench and scales effortlessly into roles like a packing station workbench, electronic assembly workbench, or even an inspection station workbench. Its minimalist yet sturdy construction makes it a favorite for teams seeking reliability without complexity.

Each unit is crafted from a robust steel workbench frame, offering long-term durability under heavy loads. The heavy-duty workbench structure ensures vibration resistance and operational stability—whether it's being used as a technician workbench in electronics or a chemical-resistant lab workbench in scientific environments. For labs or controlled spaces, the Base Series can also be upgraded to function as a cleanroom certified workbench or biological safety workbench, thanks to compatible worktops and accessories.

Ergonomics remain a core priority in the Base Series design. With height-adjustable workbench features enabled through leg extenders, users can maintain proper posture and reduce strain during long shifts. These ergonomic adjustable workbenches support a range of operators and are especially useful in multi-shift or team-based environments where one setup must accommodate multiple users.

Adaptability is where the Base Series truly excels. From linear layouts to L-configurations, this flexible configuration workbench is perfect for tight layouts or expanding workstations. It can transform into a reconfigurable workbench system as operations grow or shift—making it a smart investment for companies seeking scalability.

Workspaces that require specialized setups will benefit from the Base Series' wide range of accessories. Combine it with integrated drawers, tool holders, power strips, shelving, or monitor arms to create a customizable industrial workbench tailored for specific workflows. It easily serves as an inventory control workbench, inventory work table, or logistics workbench, streamlining processes and improving workplace organization.

For electronics manufacturing or sensitive assembly environments, the Base Series can be adapted into an ESD protected workbench, helping to prevent static discharge that can damage components. In labs or pharma environments, users can upgrade to a chemical-resistant lab workbench or cleanroom workbench for compliance and safety.

Whether it’s a maintenance workbench in a utility area, a material sorting workbench in a logistics hub, or an assembly line workbench supporting lean production methods, the Base Series provides unmatched versatility. Its modularity and strength make it an essential part of any industrial or technical workspace.

Designed for Diverse Applications

  • The Base Series is trusted across industries and departments that include:
  • Workshops: As a go-to technician workbench for repair and general tasks.
  • Electronics Labs: For use as an electronic assembly workbench with ESD protection.
  • Pharmaceutical & Cleanroom Zones: Supporting workflows as a cleanroom certified workbench or biological safety workbench.
  • Production & Assembly Areas: Acting as a factory workbench or assembly line workbench for scalable operations.
  • Packing & Shipping Areas: Serving as a packing station workbench and logistics workbench.
  • Inventory & Sorting Stations: Functioning as an inventory control workbench or material sorting workbench.

Why Furnisys Base Series?

We understand the importance of efficiency, safety, and adaptability in today’s industrial setups. That’s why our durable industrial workbench systems are designed not just for daily use—but for evolution. The Base Series is more than a table; it’s a reliable platform that supports your workflow, your people, and your productivity.

If you're seeking a modular workbench system that’s ergonomic, customizable, and ready for growth, the Base Series from Furnisys is your answer. From ESD-safe electronics stations to chemical-resistant lab benches, it's built to meet diverse needs with precision and simplicity.
