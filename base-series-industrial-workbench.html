Base Series Industrial Workbench

All purpose workbench series designed to functionally serve your basic workspace needs.

Request a consultProduct literature

Base Workbench Worksurface Options

Base workbenches come in twelve standard sizes and two heights. Choose the size, height, and worksurface, then add accessories.

  • Workbench height: 30" (76 cm) or 36" (91 cm)
  • Heavy-duty 2" x 2" tubular legs with levelling glides
  • Load capacity: 1,000 lbs. (454 kg), evenly distributed
  • Worksurface material legend:
    • PLW = Postform Laminated Particle Board
    • PELW = Postform Electrostatic Laminated Particle Board
    • GW = GI + Plyboard
    • SSW = SS + Plyboard
    • HPL = High Pressure Laminate

Base All Purpose Workbench

"L" Configuration

  • Available in multiple sizes, with a 2-piece or 3-piece top. The 3-piece top includes a diagonal corner piece.
  • Two-piece tops have all square edges. Three-piece tops in laminate and plyboard have a front radius edge.
  • Can be assembled in left-hand or right-hand orientation.
  • Eliminates the knee-space interference that occurs when two independent benches are placed in an "L".

Base Workbench Leg Extenders

  • Allows nominal height adjustment of 6" in 2" increments
  • Extender for 30" high workbench: overall heights of 32", 34", or 36"
  • Extender for 36" high workbench: overall heights of 38", 40", or 42"

Base Workbench Accessories

  • Under Shelf: For extra storage under the bench; 12" and 18" under shelves mount to leg braces
  • Fixed Heavy Gauge Footrest: Channel steel footrest fastens to leg braces
  • Back And End Stops: Steel stops fasten to the worksurface; stops extend approx. 4" above the bench top; end stops are 30" deep with rounded front corners; back stop can also attach to a riser shelf
  • Standard Casters: Adds mobility; swivel stem casters 5" x 1 1/4" replace glides on stationary legs; increases overall nominal height by 6"; includes 2 swivel and 2 locking swivel casters
  • Outlet Strip: Surface-mounts to top or bottom of worksurface; 8 outlets of 6/16 Amp (7 socket + 1 MCB), on/off switch, power cord and circuit breaker

Base Workbench Above Worksurface Accessories

  • Riser Shelf With Box Supports: 14" (36 cm) deep shelves, finished in standard or static dissipative laminate to match the worksurface; steel box supports 19" (48 cm) high and 11" (28 cm) deep
  • Electrical Panel Insert For Box Support: Includes "I5 Amp" circuit breaker, 6 sockets and power cord; can be used on one or both shelf supports; purchase with riser shelf or add later
  • LED Undershelf Light: Mounts under a shelf or cabinet; magnetic mounting; colour temperature 4000K–6000K
  • Rubber mat: 4–6 mm thick; standard colour black
  • ESD mat: 2 mm thick; includes ESD accessories listed below; standard colour grey

ESD Accessories

  • Wrist Strap and Ground Block: Mounts under worksurface; dual receptacles for 2 wrist straps; includes ESD grounding cords, ESD buttons, grounding plug and 3-way connectors
  • Ground Kits: Supplied with all electrostatic dissipative worksurfaces or shelves; includes attaching hardware

HANGING DRAWERS

  • Option 1 (IL = individual lock / NL = no lock): Steel drawer mounts under the worksurface and slides to any horizontal position; telescopic slides and integrated handles; each unit with lock and 2 keys (when locked); 16 1/2" wide x 19 1/4" deep
  • Option 2: 21 1/2" wide x 18 1/2" deep; lock not available; telescopic slides and full-length integrated handle

Base Series Industrial Workbenches – Functional Simplicity, Industrial Strength

  • Purpose-built industrial workbench for workshops, labs, warehouses and production floors; a modular platform balancing flexibility, durability and user comfort.
  • Suits repair workshops, warehouse material handling, daily factory production, packing, electronic assembly and inspection.
  • Steel frame carries heavy loads and resists vibration; can be set up for electronics technician work or chemical-resistant lab use.
  • For labs and controlled spaces, compatible worktops and accessories allow upgrades to cleanroom or biological safety use.
  • Leg extenders allow height adjustment for ergonomic use across shifts and multiple operators.
  • Works in linear or L layouts, reconfigurable as operations change; suits companies planning to scale.
  • Accessory range (drawers, tool holders, power strips, shelving, monitor arms) supports inventory control, logistics and sorting.
  • With ESD-protective worktops/accessories serves electronics manufacturing; chemical-resistant or cleanroom options suit labs and pharma.
  • Can serve maintenance, material sorting and lean assembly line roles.

Designed for Diverse Applications

  • Workshops: technician workbench for repair and general tasks
  • Electronics Labs: electronic assembly workbench with ESD protection
  • Pharmaceutical & Cleanroom Zones: cleanroom certified workbench or biological safety workbench
  • Production & Assembly Areas: factory workbench or assembly line workbench
  • Packing & Shipping Areas: packing station workbench and logistics workbench
  • Inventory & Sorting Stations: inventory control workbench or material sorting workbench

Why Furnisys Base Series?

  • Furnisys designs workbench systems for daily use and future change; the Base Series is a platform that supports workflow, people and productivity.
  • Positioned as ergonomic, customizable and scalable modular workbench, suited to ESD-safe electronics stations and chemical-resistant lab benches.

Product Overview

  • Choice of Sizes: Available in two heights, six widths, and two depths
  • Durable Worksurface Options: Plastic laminate, static dissipative laminate, or cladded worktops in stainless steel and galvanized iron
  • Leg Extenders: Adjustable height in 2" increments (up to 6" total)
  • Link System: Vertical space above the worksurface using the "Link Uprighter" system for shelves, pegboards, lighting and tool rails
  • Workstation Accessories: Bottom shelves, back and end stops, and electrical provisions
  • Heavy-Duty Construction: Supports up to 454 kg of evenly distributed load
