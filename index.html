Systems, not one-off tables.

Every Furnisys station is a set of standard parts: leg structure, worktop, storage, uprighter, pegboard and power. Change one, add one, or repeat the whole station on another line.

  • Configured to the task

    Worktop, load rating and accessories are chosen for the process, from ESD testing to heavy maintenance.

  • Predictable to buy

    Defined programs and configurations give a clear scope and repeatable results across sites.

  • Ready to expand

    Add drawers, power or bays later without replacing what you already have.
