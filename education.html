IoT Classroom

Testing microcontrollers, sensors, and prototype circuit boards requires a dedicated static-protection zone, as even minor electrostatic discharge can damage components.

The challenge

IoT labs face unique challenges: overcrowded benches, scattered tools, exposed wiring, and the constant need to reconfigure the space for different learning activities.

What we provide

Furnisys Flexi Workstations address these problems with modular configurations that can be arranged in C-shaped, back-to-back, or in-line layout to maximize floor space for both theory and hands-on practice. Each Flexi unit supports overhead cabinets for frequently used components and can be equipped with computer mounting accessories such as a CPU holder, monitor arm, and keyboard pull-out tray to keep digital systems neatly integrated into the workstation. Pegboards allow easy organization of essential tools like screwdrivers, tweezers, and pliers, while ESD mats, wrist straps, and grounding blocks enhance safety when handling sensitive electronics. For these ESD-sensitive areas, Furnisys introduces Scitec ESD Workbenches with postform ESD laminate tops, grounding points, wrist-strap anchors, and ESD-safe accessories. These benches are configured to support instruments such as voltage analyzers and oscilloscopes and provide a safe setup for soldering, debugging, and interfacing delicate hardware during hands-on experimentation. Furnisys provides tall cabinets with drawers to organize IoT experiment kits, testing instruments, and consumables. The tall cabinets offer high-volume storage for bulk items and sensitive equipment, while drawer partitions help categorize small electronic components like sensors, ICs, connectors, and wires reducing loss and improving accessibility.

FlexiOverhead cabinetPegboardScitecESD workstationTall cabinet

Outcome: Together, these solutions create a modular, safe, and well-organized IoT lab. With flexible workstations, ESD-protected benches, and a complete storage ecosystem, Furnisys enables institutions to build future-ready learning environments where students can prototype confidently and instructors can manage equipment efficiently.

3D Printing Lab

Efficient tool and material management speeds the entire printing cycle.

The challenge

3D printing labs face distinct challenges: vibrations that affect print quality, scattered tools & filament mismanagement.

What we provide

Furnisys Scitec Workbenches provide stable, vibration-resistant platforms for FDM, SLA, and other printers, with worktop options such as postform laminate and plywood to suit different requirements. Scitec workbenches can be configured with castors to create fully mobile units, and the Link uprighter system can be mounted directly on the workbench for vertical storage of tools and accessories, maximizing footprint efficiency while keeping heavy printers secure. Furnisys integrates pegboards and wall-mounted pegboards to keep scrapers, calipers, nozzle-cleaning needles, spatulas and cutters within easy reach, reducing downtime during model cleanup and maintenance. Link Workstands are available with adjustable shelves and roll holders to store and dispense filament neatly, preventing tangles and improving feed consistency for multiple machines. For inventory and consumables, Furnisys offers a complete storage ecosystem: tall cabinets with drawers, drawer partitions for organizing small parts (nozzles, fittings, spare hardware), mobile cabinets that can be fitted with different types of worktops as required, and secure lockers for personal protective equipment or valuable equipment.

ScitecLink uprighterPegboardLink workstandTall cabinetMobile cabinetLocker

Outcome: Together, these solutions create a robust and future-ready 3D printing lab. Furnisys ensures that every zone from design and slicing to printing and post-processing is organized, ergonomic, and optimized for hands-on learning. This enables students to focus on creativity and precision, while educators benefit from a clean, scalable environment built for modern additive manufacturing education.

Science Labs

Modern school science labs are dynamic learning environments where safety, functionality, and adaptability must work together seamlessly.

The challenge

Students spend long hours performing experiments, making ergonomics and organized workspaces crucial for comfort, accuracy, and learning outcomes.

What we provide

Furnisys Scitec Workbenches & Workstations provide a strong, adaptable foundation for all science disciplines. Worktops such as High-Pressure Laminate are selected according to hazards, offering durability and chemical resistance where required. Integrated utilities reduce clutter and eliminate the risks of loose cables, with horizontal power rails delivering clean, bench-level power to microscopes, hotplates, analytical balances, and detection devices, while the Link uprighter system adds vertical shelving for tools, reagents, and accessories, keeping work surfaces free and workflows efficient. In Chemistry labs, Furnisys addresses safe handling and segregation of chemicals with sink cabinets featuring chemical-resistant interiors and a choice of SS or GI sinks, suitable for cleaning, rinsing, and spill containment. Link uprighters with adjustable shelves keep commonly used chemicals and PPE off the work surface, while horizontal power rails support titration units, hotplates, and pH meters. In Biology labs, Furnisys sink cabinets with corrosion-resistant tops and proper drainage support and cleanup, and Link uprighters with adjustable shelving help manage consumables, staining materials, and incubator accessories while keeping benchtops free for observation and analysis. In Physics labs, Scitec Workbenches with Postform Laminated, High-Pressure Laminate, or Plywood worktops provide the rigidity needed for balances, interferometers, optical setups, and electrical apparatus, while bench-mounted power rails and service panels ensure clean, accessible power delivery for circuit experiments, demonstration rigs, and measurement systems. To support instructors across all science rooms, the Scitec teacher table is supplied as a standard element. It includes a hanging drawer for storing demonstration materials and an under-worktop outlet strip for powering teaching devices.

Outcome: Modular, reconfigurable systems that prioritize ergonomics; worktops and fittings selected to match chemical, thermal, and mechanical hazards; integrated routing for power, drainage, and grounding to reduce trip risks and speed setup; and a wide range of storage choices (tall cabinets, lockers, mobile cabinets, and drawer partitions) to secure consumables and instruments while keeping them accessible. These design choices reduce reconfiguration time, improve safety and maintenance, and lower lifecycle costs for educational science labs.

3D Making / Sculpting Lab

Modern 3D-making and machine workshops combine heavy fabrication, precision tooling, and rapid prototyping, so furniture must be rugged, mobile where required, and organised for safe, efficient flows.

What we provide

Furnisys Base Workbenches available with castors deliver the stable, serviceable surfaces needed across the workshop while allowing quick reconfiguration for classes or demonstrations. The Link uprighter system mounts directly to these workbenches to create vertical storage and accessory mounting, keeping tools and consumables off the worktop and improving access during busy studio sessions. Wall-mounted pegboards at dedicated tool zones provide instant visibility and retrieval for frequently used hand tools and measuring instruments, cutting downtime and improving ergonomics at the point of use. Woodworking zones benefit from Pro Workbenches with bench vises for secure clamping during sawing, routing, and hand finishing. Metalworking areas use reinforced Base Workbenches and Pro benches with dedicated vises and sturdy frames to support drilling, filing, and light machining; where heavier mills or lathes are present, benches can be anchored or specified without castors for maximum stability. Mobility and material handling are solved with Link Trolleys (pegboard + shelves) and mobile cabinets. Trolleys transport raw stock, jigs, and workpieces between prep, machine, and finishing zones, while pegboard-equipped trolleys keep essential hand tools at hand. Mobile cabinets can be fitted with different worktops for sanding, inspection, or small-part assembly and include lockable drawers for secure storage of cutters, bits, and spare parts.

Outcome: The result is faster room turnarounds, reduced tool loss and damage, safer operation around lasers and CNCs, and lower long-term maintenance costs for the lab, all while providing students with professional-grade workspaces for wood, metal, laser and CNC making.

Printmaking Lab

Modern printmaking studios bring together wet processes, mechanical presses, and fine-detail finishing, so furniture must support durability, organised workflows and user comfort.

What we provide

Furnisys addresses this by combining stable work surfaces with intelligent vertical and mobile storage: sturdy benches provide reliable support for plates and presses, while wall-mounted pegboards keep tools, rollers and consumables within easy reach, reducing unnecessary movement and improving ergonomics during long sessions. A core element of the space is the Base Cabinet with SS Worktop, which forms a practical workstation for most hands-on operations. The stainless-steel surface withstands inks, solvents and repeated cleaning, making it ideal for plate washing, ink mixing, and general prep work. Above these areas, Overhead Cabinets offer organised, easily accessible storage without occupying valuable bench space. Flexibility in studio layout comes from the use of Base Workbenches with castors, which provide mobility without compromising stability.

Outcome: Together, these Furnisys solutions create a printmaking space that is durable, well-organised and responsive to the demands of studio-based learning. SS worktop stations support wet and solvent-heavy tasks, overhead cabinets protect delicate materials, and mobile benches enable flexible teaching layouts. The result is a professional, adaptable studio environment where students can focus on technique and creativity while instructors maintain a clean, manageable and safe workspace.

Ceramics Lab

Ceramics studios face heavy wear, frequent washdowns, and dust-generating tasks.

What we provide

Students are provided with sturdy base workbenches that serve as stable platforms for sculpting and model-making, each fitted with an integrated outlet strip beneath the worktop to keep power tidy and accessible for hand tools and task lighting. Flanking the benches are base cabinets topped in stainless steel (SS), high-pressure laminate (HPL) or plywood, offering organised storage for tools, clays and consumables while delivering durable, easy-to-clean surfaces that resist wear from ceramic processes. Wet processes are supported by sink cabinets designed specifically for clay handling, glaze preparation and washout tasks. These units are available with either SS sinks or GI sinks, depending on the level of durability and resistance required. Tool access and organisation are enhanced by the Link Trolley with pegboard and adjustable shelves. Storage needs are met with tall cabinets, which protect greenware, bisqued pieces, and finished ceramics from dust and accidental impact.

Training Centre

Training centres work best when the furniture supports both learning and real technical work. For a corporate academy that teaches channel partners and employees how to assemble, service and understand Flame Arresters and Breather Valves, Furnisys created a setup that blends durability, organisation and a clean corporate look.

What we provide

In the training center the Scitec Series Workstation is designed as the primary assembly and demonstration station. Its base cabinets provide secure storage for precision tools and training supplies, while the Link uprighter adds vertical organisation with a pegboard for spanners, Allen keys, screwdrivers and other frequently used tools. A sturdy worktop fitted with a bench vise supports tightening, alignment and component breakdown during sessions. To support more flexible training styles, mobile workstations were added using mobile cabinets fitted with laminated worktops. Tall storage cabinets were placed in the training area to handle bulk storage needs. Trainees also use Base Workbenches equipped with enclosed side panels and privacy screens on three sides, with an integrated outlet strip under the worktop.

Outcome: By combining a structured instructor station, mobile support benches and learner-friendly workpoints, Furnisys created a training floor that is efficient, adaptable and comfortable to work in. Trainers can demonstrate clearly, participants can practice safely, and all tools stay organised and within reach.

Frequently asked questions.

Can one system cover every education and training space?

Yes. Work systems, storage systems and accessories share standard modules, so each space gets its own configuration within one consistent system.

Can the setup change as our operations grow?

Yes. Stations are built from standard parts, so you can add drawers, power, uprighters or bays, or relocate stations, without replacing what you already have.

Do you plan the layout before manufacturing?

For confirmed projects we carry out a site study and prepare 2D and 3D layouts and a proposal before anything is manufactured.
