Emerging labs cover robotics, mechatronics, IoT, EV systems and automation. Defined workstations help organise tools and practical activities across successive batches.

Corporate Training Room / L&D Room

Corporate training rooms support onboarding, product knowledge, technical upskilling and practical demonstrations. Different groups use the same room across successive sessions. Durable work surfaces, accessible tools and organised storage keep the space ready for assembly, demonstration and instruction.

  • Tool AccessTraining tools and aids need to remain close to the demonstration.
  • Session ChangeoversClearing and resetting can delay the next learning group.
  • Equipment ClutterShared equipment and handouts need defined storage locations.

Storage Room / Locker & Equipment Store

Campus equipment stores support workshops, classrooms, technical labs and shared learning spaces. Tools, devices, consumables, PPE and personal belongings need clearly assigned locations. Lockers and zoned cabinets support organised allocation, retrieval and return throughout the learning day.

Workspace challenges
  • Misplaced EquipmentInformal cupboards make shared devices and tools hard to track.
  • Mixed ConsumablesUnstructured storage reduces visibility and complicates retrieval.
  • Personal BelongingsStudents and staff need defined, lockable storage allocations.
  • Issue & ReturnWithout assigned locations, equipment return becomes inconsistent.
What we can provide
  • Lockers for personal or batch-wise storage
  • Lockable allocations for students and staff
  • Base cabinets for zoned equipment holding
  • Defined locations for shared campus assets

Frequently asked questions.

Can one system cover every education and training space?

Yes. Work systems, storage systems and accessories share standard modules, so each space gets its own configuration within one consistent system.

Can the setup change as our operations grow?

Yes. Stations are built from standard parts, so you can add drawers, power, uprighters or bays, or relocate stations, without replacing what you already have.

Do you plan the layout before manufacturing?

For confirmed projects we carry out a site study and prepare 2D and 3D layouts and a proposal before anything is manufactured.
