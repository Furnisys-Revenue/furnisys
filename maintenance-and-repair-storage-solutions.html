Boost Productivity and Organization with Industrial Furniture Solutions

In the maintenance and repair industry, efficiency and organization are critical to minimizing downtime and maximizing productivity. Furnisys offers a comprehensive range of industrial furniture solutions, including robust workbenches, durable tool storage units, and ergonomic accessories, all meticulously designed to meet the specific demands of your maintenance and repair operations. Our products are tailored to enhance workflow, reduce clutter, and ensure that your technicians have the tools and space they need to perform repairs efficiently.

Our solutions go beyond simple storage they transform your workspace to support optimal productivity. Whether you're repairing machinery, replacing parts, or troubleshooting, our configurable workstations and storage units are designed to maximize space and organization. With secure tool storage, ergonomic work surfaces, and confiurable options, Furnisys provides the right solutions to ensure your maintenance and repair operations run smoothly, efficiently, and safely.

Redefine Efficiency with Modular Industrial Furniture

ISSUE COUNTERS

Designed to streamline item retrieval and enhance inventory control, Furnisys's issue counter cabinets offer impeccable organization and secure storage for a multitude of fast-moving items. Maximize your workspace efficiency while ensuring easy access to essential tools and parts. With robust drawers boasting an 80kg UDL and customizable compartments, our cabinets are the ultimate solution for seamless workflow optimization.

PARTS STORAGE

When you step into a plant dealing with maintenance, one of the big headaches is often the poor spare parts management. Furnisys's storage options, like tall cabinets, drawer storage cabinets, and mobile cabinets, provide adaptable and space-saving organization solutions for challenging environments. In the repair industry, where efficiency is key, having a well-organized spare parts area can make all the difference in minimizing downtime and maximizing productivity.

REPAIR BENCHES

For the most challenging maintenance and repair tasks, you need a Furnisys's heavy-duty workbench. Our benches are built tough to handle demanding environments and long workdays, offering maximum durability and ergonomic comfort. With a variety of standard options and customizable configurations, they're ready to tackle any job, day in and day out.

TOOL BOXES

Furnisys mobile cabinets combine storage and mobility, helping technicians stay organized and efficient on the go. Featuring lockable drawers and configurable compartments, these trolleys are ideal for safely transporting tools and parts across the workshop. Engineered for stability and performance, they’re a vital part of any responsive maintenance setup.

VEHICLE REPAIR

In the vehicle repair industry, technicians are always seeking quick and convenient access to tools, parts, and supplies to provide excellent service to customers. That's where Furnisys storage cabinets, workbenches, and trolleys come in. Specifically designed to boost performance and efficiency, these solutions help technicians meet and surpass customer expectations for speedy, top-quality service. With a focus on functionality and ergonomic design, our solutions enhance on-the-job productivity, keeping your customers satisfied and coming back for more.

Mobile Cabinets

Extendable Drawers: Each drawer opens up to 90% of its length, allowing easy access to tools and materials.

Heavy-Duty Telescopic Slides: Designed to handle up to 80 kg UDL per drawer, ensuring dependable performance even under heavy loads.

Caster Mobility: Comes equipped with two swivel and two fixed heavy-duty casters, plus pedal wheel locks for stable and safe movement.

Integrated Drawer Handle: Ergonomically designed for a clean grip and smooth drawer operation.

Tubular Side Handle: Firmly fixed to the cabinet for safe handling and precise control, even when the cabinet is fully loaded.

Configurable Design: Choose from a variety of heights and drawer layouts to fit your specific workspace setup.

Locking Options: Available with or without locks, based on your security needs.

Mobile Workcenters

Drawer Storage Cabinets

Robust Drawer Construction. Reinforced telescopic slides paired with welded front and rear panels ensure high durability and consistent dimensional stability.

Smooth Operation. Precision-engineered sliding mechanism enables quiet and effortless drawer movement for daily use.

90% Drawer Extension. Drawers open up to 90% even when carrying loads up to 80 kg, offering convenient access without compromising balance.

Secure Locking System. Integrated cam lock provides controlled access to tools and components, enhancing security in dynamic workspaces.

Full-Height Sidewalls. Drawers are available in five height variants, allowing optimal use of vertical space for varying storage needs.

Ergonomic Handle Design. Integrated handles combine comfort with aesthetics, offering ease of use and a streamlined appearance.

Efficient Organization. Drawer slots support flexible partitioning, while angled top edges aid in quick and accurate label identification.
