Furnisys vs Messung.

Messung is an example of the integrator route: a project company that sources from several vendors. Here is how it compares with a configured Furnisys system, so you can choose what fits your project.

Discuss your projectCompare all options

About Messung

Messung Workplace Technology, Pune, Maharashtra

Lists AFM-PLUS ESD work tables, ESD chairs, racks and trolleys, and laboratory furniture systems for test and measurement environments.

Source: Messung product deck, as published in November 2026.

About Furnisys

Furnisys, a brand of Fabtech India, Navi Mumbai

Modular, configurable work and storage systems (Industrial System, Wall System and Lockers System), designed, manufactured and installed from our 22,000 sq. ft. facility since 2018.

When Messung may suit you

  • You need many trades managed under one contract
  • The project goes far beyond furniture
  • You have no plans to extend or change the setup

What to watch for

  • Products come from several vendors, so consistency varies
  • Configurability depends on the sources used
  • Future changes depend on the same vendors being available
  • Support is project-based and ends with the project

How a Furnisys system differs

  • One manufacturer for frames, worktops, storage and accessories
  • Process-specific configuration from standard modules
  • The same range is available years later for additions
  • Design, manufacturing and installation from one team
