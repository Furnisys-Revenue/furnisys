Base Series workbench

A multipurpose workbench series for general workspace needs, available in linear and L configurations.

Product page

Specifications

ConstructionHeavy-duty MS 2" × 2" tubular legs with levelling glides
Load capacity454 kg UDL
Heights30" (762 mm) and 36" (914 mm)
Widths36" to 96" (914–2438 mm); 84" and 96" need an additional leg
Depths30" (762 mm) and 36" (914 mm)
WorksurfacesPLW 36 mm, PELW 36 mm, GW 38 mm, SSW 38 mm, HPL 16 mm
L configuration2-piece or 3-piece top, left or right hand

Accessories and options

  • Leg extenders: height in 2" steps up to 6" (32" to 42" overall)
  • Undershelf, 12" or 18" deep
  • Fixed heavy-gauge footrest
  • Back and end stops, 4" above the top
  • Outlet strip and electrical panel inserts
  • ESD mat with wrist strap and grounding accessories
  • Hanging drawers and cabinets

Scitec Series workbench

A workbench series for diverse electrical and technical environments, with fully enclosed legs.

Product page

Specifications

Construction16 to 14 gauge MS 2" × 2" tubular legs
Load capacity454 kg UDL
Heights27½" (700 mm) and 33½" (850 mm)
Widths48" to 96" (1219–2438 mm); 84", 90" and 96" need an additional leg
Depths24", 30" and 36" (610–914 mm)
WorksurfacesPLW, PELW, GW, SSW, HPL
Configurations2 leg frames; 1 leg frame + 1 base cabinet; 2 or more base cabinets (16.85" or 22.20" wide)

Accessories and options

  • Top support rails
  • Instrument riser shelves, with power option
  • Back stops and end stops
  • Hanging cabinets and drawers
  • ESD mat and grounding accessories
  • Link uprighter accessories

Pro Series workbench

An industrial-grade workbench series for heavy and rough work environments.

Product page

Specifications

Construction16 to 14 gauge MS heavy-duty welded legs
Heights27½" to 39½" (700–1000 mm)
Widths48" to 96" (1219–2438 mm); 84", 90" and 96" need an additional leg
Depths24", 30" and 36" (610–914 mm)
WorksurfacesPLW 36 mm, GW 38 mm, SSW 38 mm
Support railRequired where leg frames or cabinets are more than 1000 mm apart
Configurations2 leg frames; 1 leg frame + 1 base cabinet; 2 or more base cabinets

Accessories and options

  • Bottom shelves, 12" deep, 68 kg UDL
  • Adjustable riser shelf assemblies
  • Stationary riser shelf, 15" deep
  • Hanging cabinets and drawers
  • Back stops and end stops

Link System

Uprighters, workstations, workstands, trolleys and flow racks. The Link uprighter mounts on Base, Pro, Scitec and Pack workbenches, or on an existing workbench.

Product page

Specifications

Uprighter framesHeavy-gauge MS starter and adder frames, notched front and back at 1" increments
Frame heights30" and 48"
Frame widths24", 30", 36", 42", 48", 60"
Sizing ruleThe worksurface must be at least 6" wider than the uprighter (e.g. 48" uprighter on a 54" top)
Trolleys48" and 60" high, plus casters
Flow racksGravity-fed part feeding with adjustable-angle shelves

Accessories and options

  • Adjustable shelves
  • Overhead light and tool rail brackets
  • Undershelf task light, 24" and 48"
  • Accessory shelf on articulating arm
  • Pegboard, privacy, marker and tack panels
  • Bin rails, tool rails and power rails
  • Overhead cabinets and overhead riser shelf

Flexi System

The most flexible system: workstations, universal carts and flow racks on a double-sided column with a macro and micro slot positioning system.

Product page

Specifications

ColumnOne macro and two micro slots on each side; one column serves back-to-back and adjacent sections
AdjustmentMacro slots in 3" steps; micro slots in 1" steps
Upright heights54", 72" and 84"
Section widths24", 30", 36", 48", 60", 72"
Outriggers15", 22" and 30" deep
Worksurfaces1" thick: PLW, PELW, plyboard, HPL (5/8"), stainless steel; 24" to 36" deep
LayoutsIn-line, back-to-back, U, X, L and T

Accessories and options

  • Back stops, 3" high
  • Rubber and ESD mats
  • Hanging drawers and file drawers
  • Steel shelves, horizontal to 30° incline
  • Overhead light brackets
  • Horizontal and vertical power rails
  • Marker boards, tack boards and utility bins
  • Data beams, air beams, cable trays and ESD ground blocks

Pack Series

Accessories that turn a Scitec or Pro workbench, base cabinets and Link components into a packing workstation.

Built onScitec or Pro workbench, base cabinets and Link System components
Leg insertsPanels that carry shelves and roll holders between the legs
Bottom shelves16" deep, accept solid dividers
Deep shelves18" or 24" deep, 68 kg UDL

Accessories and options

  • Packing bench leg inserts
  • Roll holders, leg or cabinet mounted
  • Solid dividers, 6", 10" and 18" high
  • Vertical dividers for adjustable shelves
