A tank protection specialist expands its factory

Situation

The client makes flame arresters, breather valves, tank blanketing valves, emergency relief valves, sampling valves, sight flow indicators and strainers. As part of a factory expansion, they wanted a structured layout for assembly, inspection, grinding and packaging.

Uneven floors and furniture that must move

The challenge

The trimix floor was uneven, so every table needed levelling. The client also wanted dismantlable furniture for easy relocation. Their requirement covered four assembly tables with LED lighting, rear power and lockable storage; grinding, packing and ancillary tables with 6 mm tops; compact lathe-side tables with 20–30 kg drawers; and a mobile cabinet.

How do you keep surfaces flat on a floor that isn't?

The question

How do you give every zone a stable, vibration-free surface on an uneven floor, and still be able to dismantle and move the layout later?

Five configurations from one system

The Furnisys approach

1. Assembly tables: Scitec Workstations with storage and accessories (4)

GI-cladded worktops for valve discs, springs, diaphragms and flanges; M12 × 25 threaded stud levellers; a storage base cabinet for torque wrenches, micrometers and spares; a Link Uprighter with pegboard, screwdriver, Allen key and spanner holders, marker board, bin rail, horizontal power rail and overhead LED light.

2. Grinding tables: Scitec Workbenches

GI-cladded tops for metal abrasion and grinding wheels, reinforced top support rails to resist vibration, and the same levellers.

3. Lathe-side assembly tables

Compact Scitec tables with GI-cladded tops and a combination of drawers (65 kg each) and a lockable cupboard for lathe tools, inserts and polishing paper.

4. Packaging workstation

A Scitec workstation with GI-cladded top and Link Uprighter carrying an extended roll holder, label dispenser, adjustable shelf and overhead LED light.

5. Mobile cabinet: four drawers

Lockable, castor-mounted storage for gauges, calipers and assembly tools moving between zones.

Machine, grind, assemble, pack

How the setup works together

Stems and housings are prepared at the lathe-side tables, finished at the grinding benches, assembled and calibrated at the assembly stations, then packed with labels and protective wrap at the packaging station. The mobile cabinet carries gauges and tools wherever they are needed.

What the factory received

What the configuration enabled

  • Level, vibration-free surfaces across an uneven trimix floor
  • Dismantlable furniture that can be relocated as the factory changes
  • Storage, power and light matched to each zone
  • A consistent system from machining through to packing

Frequently asked questions

How do workbenches cope with an uneven factory floor?

Threaded stud levellers (here M12 × 25 with nylon base) let each table be levelled individually.

What worktop suits grinding of valve housings?

A GI-cladded worktop with a reinforced support rail, which stands up to abrasion and vibration from bench grinders.

Can workstations be dismantled and moved?

Yes. All tables in this project were specified as dismantlable for future relocation.
