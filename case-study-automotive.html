Eight packaging workstations for a bearing manufacturer

A repeat client expanding its warehouse needed more packaging workstations. Furnisys studied the packaging process and configured Scitec workbenches with Link Uprighters and pack accessories.

Situation

The client makes high-precision bearings and actuator solutions for the automobile industry, along with maintenance products. They had used Furnisys packaging workstations before. During the expansion and renovation of their warehouse, they needed additional workstations for packaging.

The challenge

The catalogue gave an overview, but choosing the right configuration from it was difficult. The workstation had to suit the actual process: 1600 × 900 mm pallets, 2-ply rolls, stretch film, several carton types and sizes, labels and tapes, an automatic sealing machine and a weighing machine.

The question

What does a packer need within arm's reach, and what can sit above or below the worktop?

Approach

  1. Scitec Workbench

    The base of each station.

    • GI with wood cladding worktop for weighing, sealing and packing
    • Two under-worktop shelves: an upper shelf for bearings in bins or trays, a bottom shelf with dividers for cartons, film and packing materials

  2. Link Uprighter

    Mounted on the worktop to use vertical space.

    • Solid board with whiteboard for SOPs and daily instructions
    • Adjustable shelf for scissors, safety knives, tape dispensers and markers
    • Fixed tube light over the work area
    • Horizontal power rail for the sealing and weighing machines and future equipment
    • Label holder for stretch film rolls, and a monitor arm for digital tools

  3. Pallets beside each station

    Packed and sealed boxes move straight onto pallets for storage and dispatch.

How it works together

Eight identical configurations were arranged in the packaging area. Each station follows the same flow: bearings from the bins below, weighing and sealing on the worktop, cartons and film from the shelves, SOPs and tools on the uprighter, packed boxes onto the pallet beside it.

What it enabled

  • An organised workflow with tools and materials within reach, reducing time spent searching.
  • Capacity for more packaging tasks at once, supporting the expanded warehouse.
  • Lean principles in practice: less motion waste, and less waiting through integrated power and storage.

Furnisys perspective

A repeat order is where a system proves itself. The same components were configured around this client's packaging steps and repeated across eight stations.

Yes. The horizontal power rail and adjustable shelves leave room for future equipment.
