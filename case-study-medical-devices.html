An ESD-safe testing lab for medical equipment

A medical device company set up a laboratory to test high-value equipment. Furnisys configured Scitec workstations and workbenches with ESD grounding and planned the layout in 2D and 3D to fit the available space.

Situation

The client works in orthopaedics, sports medicine and advanced wound management. A new lab for testing high-value medical equipment was being set up in Mumbai, with a project management consultant and a contractor handling the fit-out. The contractor shared the furniture requirements with Furnisys.

The challenge

  • Testing tables had to be ESD-safe to protect sensitive equipment.
  • The furniture had to fit within the available space.
  • The client needed to meet its international requirements for the lab.

The question

How do you fit a full testing workflow into a fixed room while protecting every device from static?

Approach

  1. Testing tables: 7 Scitec workstations

    The primary area for day-to-day testing.

    • Instrument riser shelf with power supply for oscilloscopes, analysers, test signal generators and diagnostic PCs
    • Postform laminated worktop for hand tools, multimeters and measuring instruments
    • Base cabinet for ESD-safe tools, test leads, probes, spare components and cleaning supplies

  2. Wall-side workbenches: 4 Scitec workbenches with base cabinets

    Placed along the wall for supporting activities.

    • One bench carries a hydraulic or pneumatic testing rig for stress testing
    • Others support inspection, assembly and disassembly, and material testing
    • Base cabinets hold fasteners, fixtures, torque tools and spares

  3. ESD grounding throughout

    All Scitec workbenches and workstations were grounded for electrostatic discharge protection.

  4. 2D and 3D space planning

    Detailed visualisations placed the seven workstations and four wall benches within the room before manufacture.

How it works together

Riser shelves lift electronic instruments off the worktop, so the surface stays free for the device under test. Cabinets below separate tools by frequency of use and size. The wall benches handle the heavier and supporting tasks, keeping the central stations focused on testing.

What it enabled

  • A testing workflow that fits the available room.
  • Elevated instruments and more usable worktop.
  • Organised storage that protects sensitive instruments.
  • A layout that can take additional equipment or workflow changes.

Furnisys perspective

In a lab, the furniture is part of the test setup. Planning the layout in 3D first meant the stations fitted the room and the workflow before anything was built.

FAQ

What makes a Scitec workstation ESD-safe?

ESD-safe worktops and proper grounding of the workbench, with ESD accessories as needed for the work.

Can you plan the layout before we order?

Yes. We prepare 2D and 3D layouts based on your room and process so the configuration can be reviewed first.

Can the lab be changed later?

Yes. Shelves, power, cabinets and stations are standard components and can be added or rearranged.
