A Relocatable Heavy-Duty Workstation for On-Site Chemical Cleaning Crews

A Pro Workstation with a GI-cladded top, hanging drawers and a Link Uprighter, built to be dismantled and redeployed at customer sites for descaling and maintenance work.

Maintenance done at the customer's site

Situation

The client supplies acid-based chemical formulations for industrial cleaning and descaling, and carries out maintenance for energy, power and process plants. Their teams work on site, handling heavy components, tools and service equipment.

Strong, chemical-resistant and portable

The challenge

The workstation had to carry high loads, resist industrial fluids and chemicals, stay stable during demanding tasks, and be easy to assemble and dismantle for transport between client sites. Their existing setup had limited load capacity and no proper storage, which led to clutter and slow setups.

How do you take a proper workshop to someone else's plant?

The question

How do you give a field team a heavy-duty, organised workstation that sets up quickly in tight spaces and packs down for the next site?

A Pro Workstation configured for field service

The Furnisys approach

  • Pro legs and frame keep the bench rigid during heavy lifts and torqueing, and when handling exchanger sections or pumps
  • GI-cladded worksurface resists splashes of descaling acids and cleaning agents, prevents corrosion at impact points and spreads heavy loads
  • Hanging drawers hold hand tools, torque wrenches, calibration instruments and small jigs, away from drips and contamination
  • Link Uprighter with a pegboard and medium slotted hooks for spanners, pliers, cable cutters, hammers, strap wrenches and cordless drivers; a spanner tray, an Allen key holder and a bin strip for fasteners, O-rings, sample vials and tubing clips

Set up, service, pack down

How the setup works together

The frame and top go up quickly at the site, tools hang at elbow height, and precision instruments stay protected in the drawers. When the job ends, the modular structure dismantles for transport.

What the client gained

What the configuration enabled

  • Less bending, walking and tool searching on site
  • Visible tools and labelled drawers that support 5S
  • Chemical-resistant surfaces that protect tools and consumables
  • Faster changeovers between client sites

Modular means portable

Furnisys perspective

A bolted, modular frame is what makes a heavy-duty bench practical to move. For field teams, that portability is part of the specification.

Frequently asked questions

Can a heavy-duty workstation be moved between sites?

Yes. The Pro Workstation is modular and can be dismantled and reassembled for transport.

Which worktop resists descaling chemicals?

A GI-cladded worksurface resists acid splashes and corrosion at impact points.

Why use hanging drawers on a maintenance bench?

They keep tools and instruments directly under the work surface, protected from drips and contamination.
