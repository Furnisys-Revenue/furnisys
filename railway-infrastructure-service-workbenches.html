Zero Carbon Tech 24 Case Study (Industrial & Railway Infrastructure Services)

Client profile: Industry: Clean Technology · Product & services: Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CCUS) solutions · Function: Assembly, Storage · Furnisys services: Consultancy (product configuration) and after-sales service

Problem Statement-

Needed high-quality workbenches and workstations to assemble a new product about to launch; strict timeline — delivery within four weeks; client supplied reference drawings and requirements.

Solution

  1. Assembly Workstations: new air purifier Waro C6 requires joining about 20–25 sub-components with basic hand tools (no pneumatic tools). Four base workbenches, each with: Link uprighter; Pegboard; Horizontal power rail; Fixed tubelight with reflector; Under-worktop hanging drawer. Plus ESD accessories: wrist straps and grounding blocks.
  2. Basic Assembly Tables: pre-configured basic tables from the standard range with ESD accessories (wrist straps and grounding blocks).

Detailed data

Assembly Area: Workstation Configuration (WARO-C6 electronic and mechanical sub-assembly)

  • Standard size: 72″W x 36″D x 30″H (cost-effective, rapid deployment)
  • Pegboard: screwdrivers, pliers, wire strippers, soldering irons, multimeters
  • Light reflector: PCB soldering, wiring harness connections, microcontroller calibration
  • Horizontal Power Rail (HPR): testing power inverters, battery management systems, airflow regulators
  • Hanging drawers: miniature pumps, connectors, fuses, terminal blocks
  • Worktop: Postform Laminated worktop (PLW)
  • ESD protection: PCBs, sensors, microcontrollers

Captions: Shared Reference Drawing | Furnisys Configuration

Workbench Configuration (final assembly)

  • Standard size: 60″W x 30″D x 30″H
  • Structural assembly: main housing, fan motors, filtration units
  • Stability: integrated end stops for solar panels, battery packs, aluminium frame structures
  • Worktop: PLW (instead of SS or HPL, for cost)
  • ESD protection: digital sensors, fan motor controllers, smart filtration elements
  • Quantity: two requested; after workspace analysis Furnisys recommended eight
