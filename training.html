Product training.

The Furnisys product training used with our channel partners: the product family, what each series offers, why the series doesn't define the application, and how to configure a workstation step by step.

Module 1

The Furnisys product family.

Everything sits in two systems. The work system covers workbenches (Base, Scitec, Pro), the Link System and the Flexi System. The storage system covers base cabinets and mobile cabinets.

Module 3: the series doesn't define the application.

Each series has its own features, but none is fixed to one application. The same assembly, ESD or packaging workstation can be built on Base, Pro or Scitec, depending on the industry, the space and the load.

Module 4: assembly and kitting worked examples.

Base Series

Valve assembly workstation

Operators assembled valves with multiple hand tools, pneumatic tools and many small components. A modular workbench with integrated tool organisation and accessory support kept tools and components within easy reach as production expanded.

Scitec Series

Industrial valve assembly workstation

Operators handled precision components, fastening tools and inspection instruments. A workstation with integrated storage, lighting, power access and adjustable levelling gave a stable, reliable work surface even on uneven flooring.

Pro Series with Link uprighter

Premium bicycle assembly workstation

Technicians fitted, aligned and adjusted components with bicycle-specific tools. Workstations with integrated base cabinets and the Link uprighter system kept tools, spare parts and consumables within easy reach.

Flexi System

Jewellery packing and kitting workstation

Operators sorted and prepared jewellery components and packaging into complete kits. A Flexi universal cart with adjustable shelving and an integrated worktop maximised storage and kept materials accessible for several operators.

Link System

PCB assembly workstation

Operators soldered LED PCBs and fitted them into housings. A Link workstand with ESD protection and integrated accessories protected sensitive components and kept assembly tools within reach.

Module 5

How to configure a workstation.

Start from the enquiry: what is assembled, the tools used, the load and the space. In the training example, a manufacturer of gas detection equipment worked on non-standard wooden furniture with no ESD-safe surface, scattered tools, no component storage, poor lighting and poor ergonomics.

  1. Select the series

    Choose by application, load-carrying capacity and the dimensions required.

  2. Select the worksurface

    Choose length, depth and worktop material to suit the task.

  3. Select below-worksurface parts

    Choose 2 legs, 1 leg with a cabinet, or 2 cabinets, plus stringers, bottom shelves and hanging cabinets.

  4. Select above-worksurface accessories

    Add back and end stops, riser shelves, mats and Link uprighter components.

  5. Select the colour

    Pick one of the five standard powder coat colours.
