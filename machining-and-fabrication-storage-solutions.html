Machining and Fabrication Storage Solutions

Durable and Efficient Storage Solutions for Tools and Materials in Machining and Fabrication

Furnisys provides durable industrial furniture designed for the demanding environment of machining and fabrication. Our heavy-duty workbenches are ideal for supporting heavy tools and materials. Integrated storage solutions, such as hanging drawers and base cabinets, ensure efficient organization, whether for precision tools like drills and reamers or general materials. These workbenches enhance both productivity and safety, creating a reliable workspace.

For flexibility in tool and material transport, our trolleys are designed to easily move equipment across the workspace, ensuring an optimized flow of work. Furnisys's configurable solutions allow you to tailor layouts based on your needs, providing the versatility to adapt to various machining and fabrication applications. Whether it's organizing perishable tools, improving access to materials, or supporting heavy-duty tasks, our solutions provide precision, efficiency, and durability to meet the rigors of your industry.

QUALITY TESTING & INSPECTION

Customized Solutions for Machining Excellence Experience unparalleled precision in inspection with our customizable modular workstations designed specifically for quality control in fabrication. Versatility is key, enabling tailored solutions to meet your exact requirements. Our modular furniture and storage cabinets are engineered to maximize space efficiency, offering increased storage capacity and superior organization.

SHOP FLOOR & CELLS

As cellular manufacturing becomes more common, workers like assemblers, technicians, and engineers require better workstations. Our cutting-edge workstations, along with mobile cabinets and Accessory Systems, are perfect for cellular manufacturing setups. They're configurable to fit specific needs, offering easy access and boosting productivity for technicians on the job.
