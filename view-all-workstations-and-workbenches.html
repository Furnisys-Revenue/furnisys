Frequently asked questions.

What is an industrial workbench?

A steel workbench built for factory, workshop or lab work, rated for a stated load and fitted with a worktop, storage and accessories suited to the task. A workstation is a workbench built out for one task, with shelves, bins, power and tools at hand. Workbench vs workstation.

How much load can a Furnisys workbench carry?

The Base Series carries up to 454 kg evenly distributed load and the Pro Series up to 1,000 kg. For Scitec and Flexi, the rating for your configuration is confirmed on the drawing.

Which worktop should I choose?

Laminate for general assembly, static-dissipative laminate or an ESD mat for electronics, galvanised iron cladding for impact and die work, and stainless steel cladding for oils, chemicals and washdown. Worktops for every industry.

Can workbenches be made ESD-safe?

Yes. ESD worksurfaces with a wrist strap and ground block are available on the Scitec, Base and Flexi series. ESD workstations.

Where are Furnisys workbenches made?

They are designed and made by Fabtech India in its 22,000 sq. ft. facility in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, and supplied across India.

How do I get a layout and quotation?

Share the processes, number of operator positions, heaviest item on the bench and a floor plan. Our team returns a 2D/3D layout and drawings of the configuration. Call +91 70457 63012 or send the details online. Request a consult.
