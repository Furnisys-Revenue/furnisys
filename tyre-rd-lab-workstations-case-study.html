Seven Workstations for an Automotive Tyre R&D Lab

Scitec workbenches with hanging drawers and Link Uprighters, plus base-cabinet benches with GI tops and vices, configured for tyre sample inspection, material testing, section preparation and documentation.

An R&D lab built around tyre samples

Situation

A global automotive components and tyre manufacturer was setting up an R&D lab for tyres. They specified five workstations with defined dimensions, each for a specific task, and shared reference images for each. Two more ESD workstations were needed for sensitive electronics.

Hanging twelve tyre sections at every bench

The challenge

Tyre section samples weigh about 1 kg and measure around 6" wide. Each workstation needed pegboards with adjustable pins that could hold up to 12 sections securely and adapt to their different widths and depths. Other benches needed vices for cutting and preparation, and the electronics stations needed static control.

How do you equip one lab for very different tests?

The question

How do you configure benches for heavy sample preparation, delicate electronics and precise measurement in the same room, while keeping them visually and structurally consistent?

Starter and adder uprighters sized for each task

The Furnisys approach

1. ESD assembly workstations (two sizes)

Scitec Workbenches with hanging drawers and Link Uprighters. The starter sets carry three bin rails; the adder sets carry an overhead light, adjustable shelf, pegboard with hooks and horizontal power rail. Postform laminated tops with ESD mats and accessories.

2. Sample preparation benches

Base cabinets with GI-cladded worktops and Link Uprighters, fitted with hooks and a mounted bench vice. Two benches of one size and one of a second size were supplied.

3. Assembly and inspection workstations

Scitec Workbenches with hanging drawers and Link Uprighters carrying pegboards with bin rails, horizontal power rails and overhead lights, in a full-size and a compact configuration.

Four lab tasks, one system

How the setup works together

  • Tyre sample inspection: samples hang on the pegboard for hands-free inspection with digital calipers, micrometers, dial indicators, magnifier, barcode reader and monitor.
  • Material characterisation: FTIR, DSC and TGA work, electronic balances, clamp meter and hot air gun, with bins for reagents and consumables.
  • Section preparation: impact wrench, heavy-duty knife, bench vice, rotary cutter and cutting mat on GI-cladded tops.
  • Dimensional analysis and documentation: dial indicators, thickness gauge, torque wrench, pressure gauge, barcode printer and scanner.

What the lab received

What the configuration enabled

  • Benches matched to each test, from heavy cutting to ESD-sensitive electronics
  • Pegboard storage that holds tyre sections for inspection
  • Power, light and bins at every station
  • A consistent system across the lab, installed and supported by Furnisys

Every bench has a job

Furnisys perspective

R&D labs look uniform from the door, but each bench does different work. Starter and adder uprighters let us vary accessories bench by bench without changing the system.

Frequently asked questions

How can tyre samples be stored on a workstation?

Pegboards with adjustable pins or hooks on a Link Uprighter can hold tyre section samples for hands-free inspection. Here each station was configured for up to 12 sections of about 1 kg each.

Which worktop suits tyre section preparation?

A GI-cladded worktop on base cabinets with a mounted bench vice, which stands up to cutting and impact.

Can ESD and heavy-duty benches be in the same lab?

Yes. Both were built from Scitec workbenches, base cabinets and Link Uprighters, with ESD mats and accessories only where needed.
