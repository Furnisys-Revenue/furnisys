Give maintenance crews a bench that survives oil and heavy parts

A heavy-duty bench with a worktop chosen for grease, fluids and impact.

Industrial SystemWall System
Request this setupAll solutions

The problem

Pumps, valves and motors are stripped down on benches that warp, stain and wobble. Tools disappear in the clutter and every job takes longer than it should.

Why it happens

General workbenches are not rated for heavy loads, and their worktops absorb oil and chemicals.

How the setup comes together

  1. Choose the worktop by contamination: SS for chemicals and frequent washdown, GI for impact and grease.
  2. Fit a vise where parts are clamped for overhaul.
  3. Store tools in drawers under the bench and on the pegboard behind it.
  4. Bring parts in on a trolley, not by hand.

What changes

The bench holds up under daily maintenance, cleaning is quicker and tools stay accounted for.

The setup

  • Pro Series workbenchHeavy-duty frame with GI or SS worktop and bench vise
  • Base storage cabinetLocked drawers for tools and spares
  • Wall System pegboardFrequently used tools on the wall behind the bench
  • Stands & TrolleysCarries parts and tools between the plant and the workshop

All standard modules. Sizes, worktops and colours are chosen for your space.
