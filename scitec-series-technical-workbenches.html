Scitec Series Technical Workbenches – Engineered for Precision, Built for Electronics

The Scitec Series Technical Workbenches by Furnisys are precision-engineered workspace solutions tailored for electronics manufacturing, assembly lines, R&D labs, training centers, and cleanroom environments. Designed with an ESD-safe foundation, these modular workbench systems offer complete protection for static-sensitive components while enhancing operator comfort and productivity.

Whether you need a visual ESD inspection table, an electronic assembly workbench, or a maintenance work table, the Scitec Series delivers ergonomic, high-performance functionality with custom configurations for every technical task.

Key Features of Scitec Series Technical Workbenches

  1. Built to meet the evolving demands of modern electronic and industrial environments, each Scitec Series unit includes:
  2. Postform Electrostatic Laminate Particle Board (PELW) worktops: Ideal for sensitive operations, offering static-dissipative surfaces to prevent ESD buildup.
  3. Optional ESD matting: Enhances anti-static performance and supports safe handling of delicate electronics.

  4. Option of Integrated riser with power outlets: Ensures direct access to electricity for soldering, testing, or powering devices on your bench.

  5. Under-bench storage options: Including lockable cabinets, mild steel assembly workbenches, drawers, and hanging units for efficient inventory and tool organization.
  6. Ergonomic handles and height options: Promote user safety and ESD height adjustable workbenches.

These customizable industrial workbenches are available in various sizes and styles to support applications in everything from SMT assembly and PCB prototyping to material sorting workbenches and inspection station workbenches.

Advanced ESD Protection – The Core of Every Workbench

Electrostatic discharge (ESD) is one of the most overlooked threats in electronics production. Our Scitec Series Technical Workbenches are engineered to prevent latent defects and damage through robust ESD protection mechanisms, including:

  • Grounding points for wrist straps, mats, and tools
  • ESD bench matting and common ground points (CGP)
  • Anti-static bins and ESD-safe storage boxes
  • Ground cords and wrist bands for operator discharge

By integrating these components into our ESD assembly work benches, you create a safe and compliant industrial workstation system ready for sensitive electronics, components, and tools.

Applications of Scitec Series Technical Workbenches

  • The Scitec Series Technical Workbenches serve a wide range of industries and functions:
  • Electronics Manufacturing: Ideal for PCB assembly, surface mount technology (SMT), and component soldering.
  • Medical Device Production: For safe and clean handling of diagnostic and implantable electronics.
  • Automotive Industry: Supporting infotainment systems, battery control units, and electronic module assembly.
  • Aerospace & Defense: For assembling and testing mission-critical electronic systems.
  • Educational & Training Institutions: Used in vocational labs and technical classrooms for hands-on training with ESD protocols.
  • Repair & Testing Zones: Perfect as maintenance work tables and ESD inspection worktables for troubleshooting and diagnostics.
  • Cleanrooms & Controlled Labs: Upgradable to cleanroom certified workbench levels for contamination-sensitive areas.

Why Choose Furnisys Scitec Series?

With a focus on safety, quality, and flexibility, the Scitec Series Technical Workbenches are your go-to solution for creating scalable, static-safe, and highly functional work zones. Every component—from the anti-static ESD worktables to the adjustable industrial workbench configurations—is designed to support lean, efficient operations without compromising on reliability.

Choose from our range of mild steel ESD work benches, modular ESD work benches, and electric height-adjustable worktables, each engineered to protect valuable components and empower technicians to work faster and safer.
