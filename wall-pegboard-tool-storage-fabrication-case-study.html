Wall Pegboards for Tool Control in a Shutter and Gate Factory

Wall System pegboards in four sizes with hooks, holders and trays, organising hand tools across fabrication, assembly and installation zones.

Many parallel jobs, many hand tools

Situation

The client designs and makes automatic shutters, industrial and commercial shutters, automatic gates, boom barriers and entrance automation systems. Their plant runs several fabrication and assembly tasks in parallel, and tools also travel between bench, shop and site.

Tools that wander

The challenge

The client wanted modular wall pegboards to store spanners, screwdrivers, Allen keys and small power tool accessories in fixed, marked positions. They chose item codes from our catalogue and gave dimensions for each zone. The reasons:

  • Quick access to the right tool reduces stoppages and cycle time
  • A fixed return point reduces losses when tools move between bench, shop and site
  • A visual inventory shows missing tools at shift change, supporting 5S
  • Tool sets change between shutter and gate models, so hooks beat fixed shelves

How do you make a missing tool obvious?

The question

How do you organise frequently changing tool sets so that every tool has a home and an empty spot is noticed straight away?

Four board sizes and a set of accessories

The Furnisys approach

Pegboards in 12" × 24", 12" × 60", 30" × 24" and 30" × 60" let the client fit compact spaces and large work areas, depending on tools and operator numbers. Accessories included standard and medium slotted hooks, Allen key holders, screwdriver holders, tray assemblies and spanner trays.

Vertical storage frees the bench

How the setup works together

Tools hang on the wall near where they are used, leaving worktops clear. Trays hold small parts and consumables for assembly and servicing. Because hooks move freely, the boards adapt when tool sets change for a new product.

What the plant gained

What the configuration enabled

  • Faster access to the right tool
  • Missing or misplaced tools visible at a glance
  • Clear worktops and standard, clutter-free work areas
  • A system that adapts to changing tool sets, with after-sales support

Simple systems, daily gains

Furnisys perspective

Pegboards are one of the simplest parts of the Furnisys range, and one of the fastest to pay off in daily work. See the Wall System.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes do Furnisys pegboards come in?

This project used 12" × 24", 12" × 60", 30" × 24" and 30" × 60" boards.

How does a pegboard support 5S?

Each tool gets a fixed, visible place, so missing tools are obvious and work areas stay standardised.

Can pegboard accessories be rearranged?

Yes. Hooks, holders and trays can be moved as tool sets change.
