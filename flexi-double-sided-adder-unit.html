Flexi Double-Sided Adder Unit

"Designed to join with double-sided starter or corner units," adds capacity without increasing footprint width.

Request a consultProduct literature

Designed to join with double-sided starter or corner units, this module expands your capacity without increasing footprint width.

Expand workspace from both sides — smart, space-saving growt.

Request a Consult Product Overview

Configure your workstation to match your workflow and workspace requirements.

Easily extend your existing Double-Sided Flexi Workstation Starter Unit by adding a Double-Sided Adder. Designed to maximize space and efficiency, this unit can be accessed and used from both sides, supporting shared tools, accessories, and workflow.

Attach to your starter or corner module to create a larger, dual-access workstation that adapts to your operations.

hoose from a range of widths, depths, and heights to suit your workspace:

Widths: 24″, 30″, 36″, 48″, 60″, 72″

Depths: 24″, 28″, 30″, 34″

Heights: 54″, 72″, 84″

Assembly includes:

Adder assembly

2 footrests

4 outriggers

2 worksurface assemblies (available in PLW, PELW, HPL, or SS cladded worktops)

Double-sided power beam (15 Amp) with light switch

2 light fixture assemblies
