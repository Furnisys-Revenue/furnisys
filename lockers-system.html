Lockers System.

Steel personal lockers in two construction series, Knock Down and All Welded. Choose the series for the level of use, then configure tiers, ventilation, handles, locks and accessories for your site.

Discuss your locker projectCompare the series

Two series, one system.

Both series share the same finish, hinges, handles and accessories, so lockers stay consistent across every floor and site.

01

Knock Down series

Built tough to withstand daily wear and tear. Crafted from prime grade mild cold rolled sheet steel and assembled with rivets and/or nuts and bolts, so lockers can be transported flat, assembled on site and rearranged later. Ideal for schools, employee lockers and hospitals.

  • Spot-welded door frame construction

All Welded series

Superior durability against hard use and abuse: all components are welded into a single rigid unit for lifelong resilience, and lockers ship fully assembled in groups up to 1372 mm wide. Built for high-traffic and demanding environments.

Tiers

  • 4 & 5 tier

Features

  • Recessed handles as standard for safety and security
  • 1.6 mm full-length piano hinges
  • Continuous door strike with rubber bumpers to reduce noise
  • 2 mm door with reinforcement hat channel / door stiffener
  • Heavy-duty sheet thickness throughout
  • Ships fully assembled

Handles

Multi-point recessed handle

Mild steel recessed cup with no protrusion from the locker front. Works with a built-in combination lock or a padlock.

Single-point recessed handle

No moving parts for low maintenance, with an anti-pry latch for added security.

Door pull

Door pull with padlock hasp; standard on all box lockers.

Locks

Built-in key lock

Cam lock in flat-key or grooved-key models, with deadbolt construction.

Built-in combination lock

Numeric lock, master-key controlled, with 5 combination changes for security year to year.

Padlock

Every door is pre-punched for a padlock.

Accessories and fittings.

Slope tops

Prevent dust build-up and discourage using locker tops for storage. Available as unit slope tops, continuous slope hoods (also for retrofit) and slope top kits for the Knock Down series.

Closed bases

Front and end bases fit between the 152 mm legs for a clean, flush look and no dirt under the lockers (Knock Down series).

Zee bases

Raise lockers off the floor where there is no plinth, with a front toe space and a concealed rear flange for floor anchoring.

Interior fittings

Shelf, coat rod and zinc-plated hooks according to locker height and depth, and a polished aluminium number plate on every door.

Questions about lockers

What is the difference between Knock Down and All Welded lockers?

Knock Down lockers are assembled on site with rivets or nuts and bolts, so they ship flat and can be rearranged later. All Welded lockers are welded into one rigid unit and ship fully assembled, with heavier gauges for hard use and abuse.

How many tiers are available?

Knock Down lockers come in 1 to 6 tiers, 2-person and duplex. All Welded lockers come in 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 tiers.

Which locks can be used?

Built-in key (cam) locks, built-in combination locks with master key, or padlocks. Recessed handles keep locks from protruding beyond the door.

Which colours are available?

Five standard colours: Jet Black (RAL 9005), Slate Grey (RAL 7015), Light Grey (RAL 7035), Carmine Red (RAL 3002) and Gentian Blue (RAL 5010).

Can lockers be made accessible?

Locker configurations can be developed with reference to India's Harmonised Guidelines & Standards for Universal Accessibility, 2021, with accessible storage 300–1200 mm above the floor and handles and locks at 600–1200 mm. Compliance is subject to verification of the selected configuration and installation.
