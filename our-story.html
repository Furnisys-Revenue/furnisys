We build the systems that make work flow.

Furnisys is the furniture systems brand of Fabtech India. We design, manufacture and install modular workstations, storage and wall systems for the spaces where real work happens: production lines, labs, service bays and classrooms.

Our journeyWhy Furnisys
Made in Navi Mumbai
Systems, not one-off tables
Why we exist
“Our mission is to reshape the project-based workspace market by offering modular, functional furniture systems as standardised products, bringing the speed, scalability and reliability of product-driven solutions to industries that have traditionally depended on custom-built setups.”

Mission

To build functional furniture systems that simplify work, improve productivity and enable organisations to operate and scale with clarity.

Vision

To become a trusted, system-led workspace brand known for discipline, reliability and functional excellence across industrial, technical and institutional environments.

Built on precision, evolved for innovation.

From precision sheet metal and custom project furniture to standardised, modular systems.

  1. Origins

    Silver Technomech

    A family-owned sheet metal company known for high-precision cutting, turret punching, fabrication and product development.

  2. Projects

    Furnace

    A project business crafting custom furniture for laboratories, education, offices and manufacturing environments.

  3. Merger

    Fabtech India

    Silver Technomech and Furnace merge, combining manufacturing strength with project experience in one company.

  4. 2015

    Furnisys is launched

    A new vertical offering modular, functional furniture systems as standardised products instead of one-off custom builds.

  5. 2020

    First industrial exhibition

    Furnisys exhibits at MAHATech 2020, Pune.

  6. 2023

    Growing presence

    Furnisys exhibits at MAHATech 2023, Pune, and at the first Maharashtra Industrial Expo (MahaindX) in Navi Mumbai.

  7. Today

    One unified company

    Fabtech India operates under one identity, with Furnisys as the lead brand serving industries across India.

What we believe.

Five values guide every product decision, program and commitment we make.

Function over form

Every decision prioritises usability and performance over decoration or trend.

System thinking

Products are never sold in isolation; they are parts of one integrated system.

Discipline & clarity

Clear rules, defined programs and predictable outcomes.

Reliability

Consistency from enquiry and design to manufacturing, delivery and after-sales.

Scalability

Solutions designed to grow, expand and adapt as needs evolve.

We are not

  • A custom furniture job shop
  • A catalogue-only furniture brand
  • A lowest-price vendor
  • An aesthetics-first design studio

We are

  • A system manufacturer and system integrator
  • Organised around clearly defined programs
  • Focused on function, scalability and long-term value
  • One accountable team from design to installation
