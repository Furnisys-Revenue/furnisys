Our Story

Built on Precision, Evolved for Innovation

Where It All Began

Fabtech India was formed through the strategic merger of Silver Technomech, an established name in sheet metal manufacturing, and Furnace, a growing project business focused on offering end-to-end project-based solutions for manufacturing,labs, education & office spaces. Together, they combined experience with fresh perspective to create a stronger, more versatile entity.

Silver Technomech, a family-owned company, built its reputation on delivering high-precision sheet metal services including cutting, turret punching, fabrication, and product development. With a strong focus on quality and timely delivery, Silver Technomech catered to a wide range of industrial needs through a experienced team of engineers and process experts.

Transition from

Furnace

to

Furnisys

Our journey in workspace furniture began under the brand Furnace, crafting custom furniture for laboratories, education, office spaces and manufacturing environments. As customer needs evolved, so did we, Furnisys was born to address broader, more dynamic requirements across industries

At Furnisys, we are driven by a clear vision:

"Our mission is to reshape the project-based workspace market by offering modular, functional furniture systems as standardized products. We aim to deliver the speed, scalability, and reliability of product-driven solutions to industries that have traditionally depended on custom-built setups."

In line with this vision, we deliver configurable and scalable furniture systems that support a wide range of functions, including R&D, testing, packaging, training, and quality control. Designed for modern industrial environments, our systems enhance operational efficiency, adaptability, and long-term value.

Fabtech India Today

Unified Capabilities, Smarter Solutions

Now operating under a unified identity, Fabtech India brings together the manufacturing excellence of Silver Technomech and the systemic approach of Furnisys. This consolidation strengthens our infrastructure, deepens our expertise, and enables us to deliver sheet metal products and workspace solutions under one roof.

With the Furnisys as a brand at the forefront, we’re committed to offering smarter, faster, and more reliable solutions, designed to grow with your needs.
