From custom solutions to modular systems.

Furnisys is the Functional Furniture Systems brand of Fabtech India. We design, manufacture and install modular workstations, storage and wall systems for the spaces where real work happens: production lines, labs, service bays and classrooms.

Our journeyWhy Furnisys
Made in Navi Mumbai
Systems, not one-off tables
Why we exist
“Our mission is to reshape the project-based workspace market by offering modular, functional furniture systems as standardised products, bringing the speed, scalability and reliability of product-driven solutions to industries that have traditionally depended on custom-built setups.”

Mission

To build functional furniture systems that simplify work, improve productivity and enable organisations to operate and scale with clarity.

Vision

To become a trusted, system-led workspace brand known for discipline, reliability and functional excellence across industrial, technical and institutional environments.

Built on precision, evolved for standardisation.

From precision sheet metal and custom project furniture to modular, configurable systems.

  1. Origins

    Silver Technomech

    A family-owned sheet metal company known for high-precision cutting, turret punching, fabrication and product development.

  2. Next

    Fabtech India

    Fabtech India is established, building on Silver Technomech's precision manufacturing strength.

  3. 2018

    Furnace: the beginning

    We entered the workspace furniture industry under the brand name Furnace, creating customised solutions for the education and manufacturing sectors and serving 104+ clients.

  4. Then

    Furnisys: the evolution

    As work environments evolved, Furnace transformed into Furnisys, a brand that delivers modular, configurable and scalable systems for companies moving toward standardisation, adapting across industries and applications.

  5. 2020

    First industrial exhibition

    Furnisys exhibits at MAHATech 2020, Pune.

  6. 2023

    Growing presence

    Furnisys exhibits at MAHATech 2023, Pune, and at the first Maharashtra Industrial Expo (MahaindX) in Navi Mumbai.

  7. Today

    One unified company

    Fabtech India operates under one identity, with Furnisys as the lead brand serving industries across India.

What we believe.

Two pillars define every design decision, and three more values guide how we work with you.

Function over form

Every decision prioritises usability and performance over decoration or trend.

System thinking

Products are never sold in isolation; they are parts of one integrated system.

Discipline & clarity

Reliability

Scalability
