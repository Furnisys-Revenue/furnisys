Jewellery Assembly and Packing Stations That Doubled With the Team

Flexi Workstations with whiteboards and accessory shelves, plus Flexi Universal Carts for the storeroom, later reconfigured from five to ten users by adding parts rather than replacing benches.

A growing jewellery brand outgrowing its space

Situation

The client makes necklaces, bracelets, rings, bangles and earrings, along with watch belts and charms. Demand was rising and the facility was expanding.

Many hands, small parts, and a storeroom to organise

The challenge

They needed furniture where several employees could assemble and pack at the same time without moving around unnecessarily, and that could scale as the workforce grew. The storeroom also needed better use of space for kitting products and jewellery parts.

How do you plan for a team you haven't hired yet?

The question

How do you set up assembly and packing for five people today in a way that can serve ten tomorrow, without buying everything twice?

Flexi for the work, carts for the store

The Furnisys approach

1. Flexi Workstations (2)

Inline Flexi Workstations with a whiteboard for task tracking, accessory shelves for jewellery saws, hammers, tweezers, beading needles, reamers, wire cutters, pliers and files, a bin container for raw materials and packing essentials, and an overhead shelf for packaging materials.

2. Flexi Universal Carts (3)

Two carts with adjustable shelves organise components and raw materials in the storeroom. A third cart has a worktop, so staff can sort, assemble or check quality right where the materials are stored.

Store, sort, assemble, pack

How the setup works together

Components are kitted on the carts in the storeroom, assembled and packed at the inline workstations, and tracked on the whiteboards. Tools sit on the accessory shelves at each position.

Ten users from a five-user setup

What the configuration enabled

When the team doubled from five to ten, we reconfigured the existing Flexi Workstations into an inline back-to-back layout by adding a work surface assembly and a few shelves. No new workstation was needed.

  • Organised assembly and packing for several people at once
  • A storeroom that supports kitting
  • Capacity doubled with minimal extra investment

Scalability you can see

Furnisys perspective

This project is a simple example of what modular means in practice: the second phase used the first phase's furniture.

Frequently asked questions

Can Flexi Workstations be expanded later?

Yes. Here an inline layout for five users became a back-to-back layout for ten by adding a work surface assembly and shelves.

What tools does a jewellery assembly station need close at hand?

Saws, hammers, tweezers, beading needles, bead reamers, wire cutters, pliers and files, plus bins for raw materials and packing supplies.

What is a Flexi Universal Cart used for?

Mobile storage with adjustable shelves. With a worktop added it doubles as a small work surface for sorting or quality checks.
