Chemical-Resistant Workstations for a Pharma Wet Analysis Room

Reinforced Scitec Workstations with HPL worktops for chillers, centrifuges and ovens, a central island with Link Uprighters, and a separate balance bench, laid out after measuring the room.

Heavy instruments, frequent spills, more people

Requirement

In a pharmaceutical R&D lab, the existing wet analysis benches had failed mechanically and the workflow had outgrown the old layout. The room had to carry heavy, vibration-producing instruments (chillers, centrifuges, vacuum ovens and drying ovens), resist frequent chemical exposure and spills, and fit more active work positions without overcrowding or cable conflicts.

Furnisys measured the room and produced 2D layouts to test several arrangements: equipment-intensive benches, a balance and weighing area, and sample prep islands, each with the service aisles and clearances the lab needed.

Four zones, each with its own bench

Furnisys solution

Equipment-intensive zone

Scitec Workstations with High-Pressure Laminate (HPL) worktops for chemical resistance and easy cleaning, on reinforced frames with extra cross-bracing and load points to carry instrument weight and reduce transmitted vibration. A single upright with a vertical power rail powers several machines without cable clutter.

Central island

Base-cabinet workstations with Link Uprighters, dual overhead shelves and a horizontal power rail. Light glassware and reagent bottles go overhead; pipettes, test tubes, spares and SOP binders go in the cabinets, so the worktop stays clear for wet work.

Balance area

A dedicated Scitec Workbench with an HPL top, levelling feet and a single Link Uprighter, kept away from heavy-equipment vibration, with power and a clear cable route for balances, label printers and data logging.

Auxiliary benches

Scitec Workstations with a single overhead shelf and horizontal power rail for sample preparation, filtering, decanting, documentation and report checking.

Strength, chemical resistance and separation

Why it works

HPL tops and reinforced frames handle chemicals and mechanical stress. Upright power rails remove floor cables. Separating the balance bench from heavy equipment protects measurement accuracy. Because every bench is modular, instruments can be moved or added as new equipment is commissioned.

Frequently asked questions

Which worktop suits a pharma wet analysis room?

High-Pressure Laminate (HPL) worktops were used here for general chemical resistance and easy cleaning.

How do you protect balances from vibration in a wet lab?

Put the balance on its own bench with levelling feet, away from chillers, centrifuges and ovens; anti-vibration pads under sensitive instruments help further.

Can lab benches carry heavy equipment like centrifuges and ovens?

Yes. The equipment benches used Scitec Workstations on reinforced frames with additional cross-bracing and load points.
