Workstation Systems for an Aerospace Manufacturer

Configuring R&D Workstation Systems for a Global Aerospace Manufacturer

  • Industry: Aerospace & Defense, Product & Parts Manufacturing
  • Function: Research & Development
  • Furnisys Program: Workstation Systems, Link Uprighter System, Storage & Locker Systems

Snapshot

  • Situation: Global aircraft and aerospace systems manufacturer needed workstations and storage for several R&D zones: wire-harness preparation, sub-assembly, general engineering and testing.
  • Challenge: Fixed zone dimensions, mixed tool and instrument needs, ESD safety without slowing frequent tool access.
  • Approach: Custom-configured linear and L-shaped workstations, mobile and tall storage cabinets, and PPE lockers, all on a common Link Uprighter system.
  • Outcome: Tools, instruments and consumables at point of use in every zone, supporting 5S and continuous R&D.

Customer Context

Advanced R&D and engineering centre for aircraft and aerospace systems; wire-harness preparation and crimping, mechanical/electrical sub-assembly, and general engineering/testing each in a defined zone.

Problem Definition

Fixed dimensional limits meant off-the-shelf furniture would not fit. Needs:

  • Worktops resisting chemical exposure and tool impact, ESD-safe in electronics zones, in line with AS9100.
  • Overhead and point-of-use storage for tools and test hardware.
  • Task lighting for precision crimping, inspection and assembly.
  • Pneumatic hose routing, waste disposal, secure lockable storage for high-value test equipment.
  • Furniture built to exact client dimensions.

System Approach

Custom-configuration route, but every workstation on the Link Uprighter platform so tool organisation, lighting and storage stayed consistent.

Execution

  • Linear Workstations (wire-harnessing, crimping, structured assembly): GI-clad worktops; ESD-compatible provisions; rear Link Uprighter with pegboards for crimping tools, cutters, torque tools; spanner trays, Allen-key holders, bin strips; overhead cabinets with under-cabinet task lighting; air hose attachment points and swing-arm tool rail; custom base cabinets and mobile cabinets with recessed handles and lockable heavy-duty castors.
  • L-Shaped Workstations (sub-assembly, ESD-sensitive electronics): postform laminated worktops with ESD mats; L-shape following existing layout; corner overhead cabinet; task lighting, pneumatic points, custom base cabinets.
  • Storage & Lockers: Tall cabinets with adjustable shelves, drawers and door-activated LED lighting; perforated bodies for hooks and bin strips; personal lockers with hanging rods and multi-point locks; mobile cabinet with Link Uprighter, integrated power module and castors rated to 240 kg.

Outcome

Each unit matched to a specific function; tools at point of use; reinforced 5S in a continuous R&D, assembly and testing facility.

What This Means for Aerospace Manufacturing in India

India's aerospace base is expanding (e.g., Mahindra's incorporation of Novavayu Aerospace); new facilities face the same questions — ESD-sensitive assembly, point-of-use tools, scalable storage. Furnisys systems are designed for established global R&D centres and new domestic entrants.

System Continuity

Link Uprighter platform lets the client extend pegboards, overhead storage, task lighting and mobile cabinets into new zones without redesign.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What furniture do aerospace R&D and wire-harness assembly areas need? — chemical/impact-resistant worktops, ESD-compatible surfaces, integrated task lighting, pegboard tool organisation, secure storage for test equipment.
  • What is an ESD-compatible workstation, and why does aerospace R&D need one? — dissipates static that could damage control boards and connectors.
  • How do modular workstation systems support 5S in engineering labs? — fixed visible locations via pegboards, bin strips, labelled trays.
  • Can workstation systems be customized to a specific facility layout? — Yes; custom-sized worktops, cabinets and corner units on the same platform.
  • What storage protects high-value test equipment in R&D centers? — tall lockable cabinets with adjustable shelving, multi-point locks and door-activated interior lighting.
