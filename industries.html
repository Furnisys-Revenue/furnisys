Industries

The work done in a space decides its furniture. These are the industries we configure for, the spaces we see most often, and what those spaces need.

Manufacturing

Spaces: Electronic assembly, machining and fabrication, maintenance and repair, and medical manufacturing.

What these spaces need: Flexible and ESD-safe workstation layouts, organised tool storage, faster changeovers and the same standard across every line.

Follow on LinkedIn

Aerospace & Aviation

Spaces: Precision assembly, MRO hangars and component workshops.

What these spaces need: Standardised workstations, tool traceability and ergonomic setups for long-duration precision work.

Follow on LinkedIn

Automotive & Auto Services

Spaces: Assembly lines, component plants, packaging areas and service workshops.

What these spaces need: Standard workstations across lines, faster assembly cycles, ergonomic operator setups and lean layouts.

Follow on LinkedIn

Oil & Gas

Spaces: Refinery maintenance workshops, field repair units and tool rooms.

What these spaces need: Heavy-duty workstations, organised maintenance setups and tool traceability for long-life infrastructure.

Follow on LinkedIn

Power Generation

Spaces: Plant maintenance, turbine repair workshops and electrical labs.

What these spaces need: Organised maintenance systems, heavy-duty workstations and tool management that keep repair work moving.

Follow on LinkedIn

Agriculture & Outdoor

Spaces: Farm-equipment service centres, repair workshops and spare-part stores.

What these spaces need: Durable workbenches, mobile tool storage and organised spares for equipment servicing.

Follow on LinkedIn

Construction

Spaces: Site workshops and equipment maintenance areas.

What these spaces need: On-site repair setups, mobile workstations and organised tool management.

Follow on LinkedIn

Jobsite Solutions

Spaces: Temporary and remote project sites.

What these spaces need: Mobile maintenance setups and organised tool systems, so the workshop moves with the site.

Metalworking

Spaces: Fabrication shops, mills and tool rooms.

What these spaces need: Heavy-duty benches with impact-resistant worktops, organised workstations and tool management.

Follow on LinkedIn

Museums, Libraries & Archives

Spaces: Archive rooms, conservation labs, stores and reading rooms.

What these spaces need: Organised archival storage, safe handling workstations and ergonomic research tables.

Tool Crib & Point of Use

Spaces: Tool cribs, issue counters and point-of-use storage on the shop floor.

What these spaces need: Tools organised where they are used, easier access and less time spent searching.

Government, Military & Law Enforcement

Spaces: Workshops, armouries, R&D labs and stores.

What these spaces need: Durable, standardised setups and organised tool systems built for long service.

Education & Training

Spaces: ITIs, vocational labs, engineering colleges, art and design schools, and school science labs.

What these spaces need: Industry-like training setups, multi-use workbenches and lab infrastructure that scales with intake.

Healthcare

Spaces: Hospitals, clinics, medical device labs and equipment testing rooms.

What these spaces need: ESD-safe testing workstations, organised instrument storage and easy-to-clean surfaces.

Gems & Jewellery

Spaces: Jewellery manufacturing: wire drawing, chain making, diamond cutting, plating, finishing and R&D.

What these spaces need: Process-specific workstations, from impact-resistant benches for hammering to precision benches for finishing.

Don't see your industry?

Describe the work and the space. Our systems are configured by task, so most environments can be planned the same way.
