A Twelve-User ESD Lab for EV Charger R&D

Back-to-back Flexi Workstations in a starter and adder layout with ESD mats, hanging drawers, angle shelves and power rails, set up after a plant renovation for EV charging station development.

A plant turned into an electrical testing lab

Situation

The client builds battery testing and production equipment, DC fast chargers for electric vehicles and hydrogen power equipment. As part of a renovation, they demolished an existing plant area to build an R&D centre for EV charging stations: an electrical testing lab for 12 people.

Many tasks, sensitive electronics, one room

The challenge

The lab would host assembly, disassembly, measurement, testing and quality control. The furniture had to be modular, with drawers for tools and instruments, practical accessories and ESD protection for sensitive electronics.

How do you seat twelve engineers without crowding the lab?

The question

How do you fit twelve ESD-safe workstations into one room so the team can collaborate, and still leave space for equipment and expansion?

Back-to-back Flexi, built from starters and adders

The Furnisys approach

Flexi Workstation layout

Each side uses one starter and two adders to seat six people, mirrored back to back for twelve users in total. Each position has:

  • Hanging drawers for measuring tools such as vernier calipers and micrometers
  • A fixed tube light assembly
  • A variable angle shelf for multimeters, oscilloscopes and battery testers
  • A horizontal power rail
  • A pegboard with whiteboard for tools and notes
  • A postform laminated top with ESD mat, common point ground block, wrist straps and grounding blocks

A shared spine with personal workspaces

How the setup works together

The back-to-back spine carries power and lighting for both sides. Instruments sit on angled shelves at eye level, hand tools hang on the pegboard, and fasteners go in bins. The whiteboard holds instructions and test notes for each bench.

Benefits the client reported

What the configuration enabled

  • Space: a compact, back-to-back layout that leaves room for equipment
  • Flexibility: six users per side, collaborative and easy to reconfigure
  • Cost: shared starter and adder frames lowered the initial investment, and more adders can be added later
  • Safety: ESD mats, wrist straps and grounding blocks protect sensitive electronics

Starter-adder pays twice

Furnisys perspective

Shared frames reduce cost on day one and make expansion simple later. For a growing R&D team, that matters as much as the bench itself.

Frequently asked questions

How many users can a back-to-back Flexi layout seat?

In this lab, one starter and two adders per side seated six people per side, twelve in total.

What ESD accessories does an electronics R&D bench need?

An ESD mat, common point ground block, wrist straps and grounding blocks were fitted at each position here.

Can the lab be expanded later?

Yes. Further Flexi adders can be added to the existing starters without replacing the layout.
