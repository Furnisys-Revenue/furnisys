Mobile Drawer Cabinets for Automotive Mechatronics Components

A six-drawer Furnisys Mobile Cabinet, configured with an aggregator partner for a leading automobile manufacturer, so technicians can carry actuators, switches and small hardware between workstations.

Mechatronics parts that move with the work

Situation

An aggregator partner working with a leading automobile manufacturer approached Furnisys. The manufacturer builds advanced automotive systems from mechatronics components: parts that are electronically sensitive and mechanically varied at the same time.

Technicians use these parts across several workstations during active operations, so storage had to travel with them rather than sit in one corner of the floor.

Many small, different parts, and none can go missing

The challenge

The parts range from solenoid and rotary actuators to pushbuttons, rotary knobs and touchscreen modules, plus the clamps, clips and PCB standoffs that hold everything together.

Loose bins and shared trays lead to mixed-up parts, damaged components and time lost searching. The manufacturer needed storage that kept each type of part in its own place, protected it from damage, and could be moved to wherever the work was happening.

How do you keep varied mechatronics parts organised while they move around the floor?

The question

How do you give technicians secure, organised access to dozens of different components without tying them to a fixed store?

A six-drawer Mobile Cabinet, configured for the parts list

The Furnisys approach

Furnisys configured a Mobile Cabinet with six drawers, with a trolley handle and castors so a technician can roll it from station to station.

The drawers were allotted by part family:

  • Actuators: solenoid, rotary, linear and pneumatic units
  • Switches and HMI components: pushbuttons, toggle switches, sliders, rotary knobs and touchscreen modules
  • Fasteners and hardware: clamps, clips, PCB standoffs and quick-connect components

Furnisys also gave the aggregator partner reference drawings and pre-configured layout suggestions, which made the selection quick and kept the solution application-ready.

The store comes to the workstation

How the setup works together

The technician loads the cabinet with the parts needed for the job, wheels it to the workstation and works from the open drawers. Because each drawer holds one part family, it is obvious what is missing before the job starts and where each part goes back afterwards.

What the client received

What the configuration enabled

  • Organised storage for actuators, switches, HMI parts and small hardware
  • Parts that travel with the technician across workstations
  • Less handling damage and fewer misplaced components
  • A configuration the aggregator partner could select from reference drawings

Mobile storage is part of the workstation

Furnisys perspective

When parts are used at several stations, a mobile cabinet does the work of a small store. Planning the drawers around the parts list is what keeps it organised after months of use.

Frequently asked questions

What storage suits automotive mechatronics components?

A mobile drawer cabinet keeps sensitive and varied parts such as actuators, switches, HMI modules and small hardware sorted by drawer, and moves with technicians between workstations.

How many drawers did this mobile cabinet have?

Six drawers, allotted to actuators, switches and HMI components, and fasteners and miscellaneous hardware.

Can Furnisys work through an aggregator or channel partner?

Yes. In this project Furnisys supplied reference drawings and pre-configured layout suggestions to an aggregator partner serving the automobile manufacturer.
