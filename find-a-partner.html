Find a Furnisys partner.

Furnisys works with channel partners, consultants and system integrators across India, so you get local support backed by our design and manufacturing team in Navi Mumbai.

Who our partners are.

Channel partners and dealers

Regional partners who stock, sell and support Furnisys systems close to your facility.

Consultants and architects

Workspace and facility designers who specify Furnisys systems in their projects.

System integrators

Integrators who combine Furnisys workstations with automation, tooling and electrical systems.

How it works.

  1. Tell us where you are

    Share your city, industry and the space you are planning.

  2. We match a partner

    We connect you with the partner who serves your region and application.

  3. Plan with local support

    Your partner works with our team on configuration, delivery and installation.

Discuss your project

Tell us about the facility and the scope. A workspace specialist reviews every brief and contacts you to discuss next steps.

Best suited to new facilities, expansions and multi-station upgrades.
