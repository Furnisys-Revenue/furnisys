Speed up packing and dispatch

A packing bench with materials fed from behind and tools above.

Industrial System
Request this setupAll solutions

The problem

Packers walk to fetch cartons, tape and labels, and orders back up at the end of the line.

Why it happens

Packing materials are stored away from the bench, and the station is not laid out in the order the work is done.

How the setup comes together

  1. Lay out the bench in the order of packing: product, carton, fill, seal, label.
  2. Mount rolls and cutters above the worktop.
  3. Feed cartons and products from a flow rack behind the packer.
  4. Move packed goods on a trolley to dispatch.

What changes

Packers stay at the bench, materials are always at hand and dispatch keeps pace with production.

The setup

  • Link Packing AccessoriesRoll holders, cutters and label holders mounted on the bench
  • Base Series workbenchPacking surface at the right working height
  • Link Flow RacksFeeds products and packing materials to the packer
  • Stands & TrolleysTakes packed goods to dispatch

All standard modules. Sizes, worktops and colours are chosen for your space.
