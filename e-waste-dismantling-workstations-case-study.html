Thirty-Two Dismantling Stations for an E-Waste Co-Working Space

Base Workbenches and Link Workstands with shelves and power rails, configured for micro-entrepreneurs dismantling and segregating electronic waste.

Legal, equipped workspace for recyclers

Situation

Micro-entrepreneurs in e-waste recycling often lack access to legal workspaces, proper tools and formal support. The client set out to change that by building co-working spaces with the tools, equipment and services these recyclers need, along with a research and real-world lab.

Finding a partner who could scale

The challenge

Local vendors could not supply scalable, consistent, configurable furniture, and the client was unhappy with their lack of a solution-focused approach. They needed a partner who understood the work and could deliver at volume.

How do you set up dismantling stations that keep materials separate?

The question

How do you equip dozens of recyclers with sturdy benches, power and organised segregation, so copper, aluminium, plastics and hazardous parts never end up in the same bin?

Sturdy benches with segregation alongside

The Furnisys approach

Base Workbenches (32)

Robust benches for dismantling everything from mobile phones to CPUs and hard drives, stable under hammers and heavier tools.

Link Workstands (3)

Placed beside or facing the benches, with shelves and horizontal power rails. They hold bins and containers for circuit boards, wires, plastics and metals, and provide power at the station.

Tools and safety

Each station accommodates hammers, screwdrivers, pliers, wire cutters, wrenches and precision tools, along with gloves, safety goggles and anti-static mats.

Dismantle on the bench, segregate beside it

How the setup works together

Devices are opened on the workbench and each recovered material goes straight into its bin on the workstand. Workers do not leave the station to sort, and the floor stays clear.

What the space gained

What the configuration enabled

  • 32 consistent dismantling stations for micro-entrepreneurs
  • Clear segregation of recyclable and hazardous materials
  • Power and tools at the point of use
  • A modular layout that can grow with the co-working space

Furniture for the circular economy

Furnisys perspective

Recovering more material from e-waste depends on good segregation, and good segregation depends on having a bin within reach. Simple, standard workstations make that possible at scale.

Frequently asked questions

What does an e-waste dismantling workstation need?

A sturdy bench, power at the station, bins for separating copper, aluminium, plastic and hazardous waste, and space for hand tools and safety gear.

Why pair workbenches with Link Workstands?

The workstand sits beside the bench and holds bins and power, so materials are segregated immediately without leaving the station.

Can Furnisys supply large numbers of identical stations?

Yes. This project used 32 Base Workbenches built from standard modules.
