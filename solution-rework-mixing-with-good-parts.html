Home / Resources / Solutions library Repair and rework

Keep rework and rejected parts away from good production

A dedicated rework bench with clear segregation for each status.

Industrial SystemWall System
Request this setupAll solutions

The problem

Rework is handled wherever there is space. Rejected parts, parts under repair and parts ready for release end up on the same surface, and some go back into circulation unfinished.

Why it happens

There is no defined rework area and no storage that separates parts by status.

How the setup comes together

  1. Set the rework bench apart from the production line.
  2. Label drawers or bins for each status: rejected, under repair, ready for release.
  3. Move parts only on the trolley, so nothing is carried back by hand.
  4. Keep repair tools on the pegboard so the rework area does not borrow from the line.

What changes

Non-conforming parts stay contained, repair is faster and nothing unfinished goes back to the line.

The setup

  • Pro Series workbenchHeavy-duty bench with SS or GI worktop for dirty or fluid-prone repair
  • Base storage cabinetSeparate drawers for rejected, under repair and released parts
  • Stands & TrolleysMoves parts between the line and the rework area
  • Wall System pegboardRepair tools kept at the bench

All standard modules. Sizes, worktops and colours are chosen for your space.
