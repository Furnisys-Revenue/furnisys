Expert Partner Program

Are you a consultant focused on Lean, Ergonomics, Process Engineering, or Space Planning? Our Expert Partner Program is tailored for professionals committed to enhancing customer efficiency through strategic consulting.

Partnering with Furnisys provides access to our system-based furniture designs, empowering you to apply your concepts more effectively. With our modular solutions, you can seamlessly integrate your strategies into client workspaces, boosting productivity and optimizing space.

Join us to leverage innovative systems for outstanding client results.

Integrator Partner Program

For industry-based system integrators, our Integration Program offers a unique opportunity to enhance your service offerings by incorporating Furnisys' system-based furniture solutions. By integrating our modular and adaptable furniture systems with your existing services, you can deliver a more unified and effective solution to your customers.

This collaboration not only accelerates business agility but also drives workplace transformation, helping your clients achieve a more efficient and optimized operational environment. Join our Integration Program and take your solutions to the next level.​

Aggregator Partner Program

Are you a dealer, distributor, or reseller catering to industrial or infrastructure-based clients? Our Aggregator Partner Program is crafted for channel partners looking to expand their product portfolio with reliable, system-based industrial furniture.

As an Aggregator Partner, you get access to our modular furniture range designed for workspaces in manufacturing, R&D, education, and more. With comprehensive product support and marketing collateral, you can confidently offer Furnisys solutions to your customers—backed by quality, flexibility, and functionality.

Join our Aggregator Partner Program to enhance your offerings and grow your business with proven workspace systems.

Referral Partner Program

Do you have direct access to industries like Manufacturing, Utilities, or Education? Our Referral Partner Program is ideal for vendors, users, and industry insiders looking to earn commissions by introducing our innovative furniture systems to their network.

By partnering with Furnisys, you can help your audience optimize their workspaces while benefiting from a lucrative commission structure.

Join our Referral Partner Program and grow your business by sharing our solutions.​
