Choose your program.

Four ways to work with us, depending on how you reach customers.

Aggregator partner

Dealers, distributors and resellers serving industrial and infrastructure customers.

  • Add modular workstations, storage and lockers to your range
  • Product support and marketing collateral
  • Layouts prepared by our design team

Expert partner

Consultants in lean, ergonomics, process engineering and space planning.

  • Apply your methods with system-based furniture
  • Configurations that match your recommendations
  • Better productivity and space use for your clients

Integrator partner

System integrators delivering automation, tooling or electrical projects.

  • Offer one unified solution, furniture included
  • Workstations ready for power, air and fixtures
  • Faster turnkey delivery for your projects

Referral partner

Vendors, users and industry insiders with access to manufacturing, utilities or education.

  • Introduce Furnisys to your network
  • Earn a commission on referred business
  • No selling or stocking needed
