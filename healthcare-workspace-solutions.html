Workspace overview

Laboratory & Sample Preparation

Hospital and diagnostic laboratories are where biological samples, reagents and test materials are processed. These are structured, process-driven environments where bench layout, surface hygiene and storage discipline directly affect result accuracy and cross-contamination risk. Workbenches here support activities from sample receipt and centrifugation to analysis preparation and instrument support.

Workspace challenges
  • Chemical ExposureSurfaces must withstand reagents and routine disinfectants.
  • Frequent CleaningWorktops should support rigorous cleaning without deterioration.
  • Organized StorageConsumables, reagents and instruments need designated storage.
  • ComplianceWorkspace design must support NABL and NABH requirements.
What we can provide
  • Laboratory workbenches with SS or HPL worktops
  • Under-bench base cabinets for reagent and consumable storage

Operational outcome
  • Clean & hygienic work surfaces
  • Organized reagent storage
  • Efficient sample preparation
  • Audit-ready laboratory environment

Clean Utility & Supply Room

Clean utility rooms are dedicated support spaces where sterile consumables, dressings, IV fluids and clinical supplies are stored and prepared for use. These environments require high standards of cleanliness, organised storage and clear separation from contaminated areas to support safe patient care.

Workspace challenges
  • Hygiene & CleanabilitySurfaces must support frequent cleaning and disinfection.
  • Cross-Contamination ControlClear separation between clean and dirty workflows is essential.
  • Organized StorageConsumables should be systematically stored for quick identification and replenishment.
  • ComplianceWorkspace organisation must support NABH infection control requirements.
What we can provide
  • Flow racks and Flexi system
  • Surfaces configured for easy disinfection and daily cleaning routines
Flexi systemFlow rack
Operational outcome
  • Hygienic preparation environment
  • Organised consumable storage
  • Faster stock access
  • Audit-ready clean utility room

Biomedical Equipment Maintenance Workshop

Hospitals above a threshold bed capacity maintain in-house biomedical engineering teams responsible for the repair, calibration and preventive maintenance of patient monitors, infusion pumps, ECG machines, defibrillators and other clinical equipment. This function operates from a dedicated technical workshop that serves the entire facility.

Workspace challenges
  • Unorganised ToolsScattered tools slow maintenance and servicing activities.
  • Poor TraceabilitySpare parts and maintenance records become difficult to manage.
  • Heavy Equipment HandlingWorkstations must safely support servicing of medical devices.
  • Equipment DowntimeInefficient workspaces increase turnaround time for critical equipment.
What we can provide
  • Heavy-duty workbenches with PLW, PELW or SS worktops
  • Under-bench base cabinets
Base cabinetWorktops
Operational outcome
  • Faster equipment servicing
  • Organised tool management
  • Improved spare traceability
  • Compliance-ready maintenance workshop

Clinical Equipment & Consumable Store

Clinical stores manage medical consumables, equipment, spare parts and patient care supplies. Efficient storage systems are essential for maintaining stock visibility, improving retrieval times and ensuring uninterrupted support to clinical departments.

Workspace challenges
  • Poor Stock OrganisationUnstructured storage leads to misplaced or expired inventory.
  • Limited VisibilityDifficulty locating supplies delays daily operations.
  • Inventory LossImproper storage increases wastage and stock discrepancies.
  • ComplianceStorage infrastructure must support organised inventory management.

  • Full-height storage walls with cabinets
  • Configured for easy cleaning and systematic stock rotation

  • Reduced inventory loss

PPE Preparation & Issue Room

PPE preparation rooms ensure healthcare staff have quick, organised access to personal protective equipment before entering clinical environments. These spaces play a critical role in infection prevention by supporting proper storage, replenishment and compliance.

Workspace challenges
  • Disorganised PPE StoragePoor organisation slows access during routine and emergency situations.
  • Stock VisibilityConsumables must be easy to monitor and replenish.
  • High-Demand ReadinessWorkspaces should support rapid response during outbreaks and surge periods.
  • Infection ControlStorage systems must reinforce safe donning practices and contamination control.
What we can provide
  • Locker systems for individual PPE allocation and staff storage
  • Configurations that support clean, organised access and easy restocking

Frequently asked questions.

Can one system cover every healthcare space?

Yes. Work systems, storage systems and accessories share standard modules, so each space gets its own configuration within one consistent system.

Can the setup change as our operations grow?

Yes. Stations are built from standard parts, so you can add drawers, power, uprighters or bays, or relocate stations, without replacing what you already have.

Do you plan the layout before manufacturing?

For confirmed projects we carry out a site study and prepare 2D and 3D layouts and a proposal before anything is manufactured.
