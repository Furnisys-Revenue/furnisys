Laboratory & Sample Preparation Area

Hospital and diagnostic laboratories are where biological samples, reagents, and test materials are processed. These are structured, process-driven environments where bench layout, surface hygiene, and storage discipline directly affect result accuracy and cross-contamination risk. Workbenches in these areas support activities from sample receipt and centrifugation to analysis preparation and instrument support.

NABL accreditation under ISO 15189 sets standards for medical laboratory quality and competence, including workspace layout and surface integrity. NABH standards also address laboratory environment requirements for accredited hospitals.
The challenge

Standard office furniture cannot withstand the chemical exposure, repeated disinfection, and load demands of a working lab. Inadequate bench depth, poor drainage provision, and lack of overhead storage disrupt workflow and create compliance gaps during NABL or hospital accreditation audits.

What we provide

We provide laboratory workbenches with SS or HPL worktops suited for chemical and biological environments, under-bench base cabinets for reagent and consumable storage, overhead cabinets for organised access, and sink cabinets where drainage requirements exist.

Base cabinetOverhead cabinetSink cabinet

Outcome: A compliant, cleanable laboratory workspace that supports sample processing accuracy, organised reagent storage, and audit-ready bench conditions.

Clean Utility & Supply Room

Clean utility rooms are defined support spaces within hospital wards, OPDs, and day-surgery units where sterile consumables, dressings, IV fluids, and clinical supplies are stored and prepared for use. These rooms must be maintained at a higher hygiene level than general areas, with clear separation from dirty zones.

NABH accreditation standards for hospitals require dedicated, organised clean utility areas as part of infection prevention and control protocols. MoHFW (Ministry of Health and Family Welfare) facility guidelines also support defined clean support room requirements.
The challenge

In many facilities, clean utility rooms are under-designed: consumables are stacked without proper segregation, shelving is improvised, and surfaces are difficult to clean thoroughly. This creates infection risk, stock management problems, and repeated findings during NABH assessments.

What we provide

We provide base cabinets with HPL or SS worktops for surface preparation, overhead cabinets for organised consumable storage, flow racks and flexi system. All surfaces are configured for easy disinfection and daily cleaning routines.

Base cabinetOverhead cabinetFlow rackFlexi

Outcome: An organised, cleanable supply room that supports infection control protocols, faster stock access, and consistent NABH audit readiness.

Biomedical Equipment Maintenance Workshop

Hospitals above a threshold bed capacity maintain in-house biomedical engineering teams responsible for the repair, calibration, and preventive maintenance of patient monitors, infusion pumps, ECG machines, defibrillators, and other clinical equipment. This function operates from a dedicated technical workshop that serves the entire facility.

NABH standards require documented maintenance programmes for all medical equipment, supported by dedicated workshop facilities. The Clinical Establishments Act and MoHFW guidelines also support the need for organised in-house technical maintenance capacity in licensed hospitals.
The challenge

Without a properly configured workshop, biomedical technicians improvise on unsuitable benches, tools are unorganised, and equipment under repair is tracked poorly. This slows turnaround times for critical medical equipment and creates gaps in maintenance records, a direct risk factor during NABH or accreditation reviews.

What we provide

We provide heavy-duty workbenches with PLW or PELW or SS worktops for equipment servicing and repair, under-bench base cabinets and tool storage for organised workshop layout, and overhead cabinets for spare parts, documentation, and consumable management. Mobile cabinets allow the technician to take organised tool sets to equipment locations across the hospital.

ScitecBase cabinetOverhead cabinetMobile cabinet

Outcome: A functional biomedical maintenance workshop that supports faster equipment turnaround, organised tool and spare management, and documentable maintenance compliance.

Clinical Equipment & Consumable Store

Hospitals and large clinics maintain dedicated stores for bulky clinical consumables, medical equipment, spare parts, and facility items including linen, single-use devices, and patient care accessories. These are high-throughput spaces where stock movement, visibility, and organisation directly affect ward supply reliability.

NABH standards for hospital operations require organised stores management as part of broader infection control and supply chain compliance. MoHFW facility guidelines for hospitals support dedicated, defined storage infrastructure.
The challenge

Poorly organised clinical stores lead to stock losses, expired consumables reaching wards, and time wasted locating items during urgent situations. Improvised storage (cardboard boxes on the floor, overloaded shelving) is a recurring non-conformance finding in hospital accreditation reviews.

What we provide

We provide full-height storage walls and modular base cabinet systems for high-density consumable and equipment storage. All units are configured for easy cleaning and systematic stock rotation.

Storage wallBase cabinet

Outcome: An organised clinical store that reduces stock loss, supports controlled issue and return, and demonstrates disciplined stores management during accreditation assessments.

PPE Preparation & Issue Room

In hospitals with operating theatres, isolation wards, ICUs, or active infection control programmes, dedicated PPE preparation and issue rooms serve as controlled points where staff store personal protective equipment. These rooms are part of the facility's infection prevention pathway and require organised, accessible storage for gloves, masks, gowns, eye protection, and related consumables.

NABH Infection Prevention and Control standards require defined PPE management processes including storage, availability, and staff training. MoHFW and WHO IPC guidance used in Indian hospital design also supports dedicated, organised PPE storage and issue points.
The challenge

When PPE is stored inconsistently or without clear organisation, donning compliance suffers and stock cannot be managed effectively during high-demand situations such as outbreaks or surge periods. A poorly organised PPE room becomes a weak link in the infection control chain.

What we provide

We provide locker systems for individual PPE allocation and staff storage, base cabinets and overhead cabinets for consumable PPE stock management. All configurations support clean, organised access and easy stock restocking.

Outcome: An organised PPE issue room that supports consistent donning compliance, visible stock management, and faster response during high-demand or outbreak conditions.

Which standards do healthcare workspaces need to support?

NABH standards also address laboratory environment requirements for accredited hospitals.. We configure worktops, grounding, storage and segregation to support them.

Can one system cover every healthcare space?

Yes. Work systems, storage systems and accessories share standard modules, so each space gets its own configuration within one consistent system.

Can the setup change as our operations grow?

Yes. Stations are built from standard parts, so you can add drawers, power, uprighters or bays, or relocate stations, without replacing what you already have.

Do you plan the layout before manufacturing?
