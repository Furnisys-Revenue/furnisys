Ordering & pricing

How can I get assistance in designing my workspace and placing an order?

Share your project brief through our contact form. A workspace specialist reviews your requirements and, for confirmed projects, our team assesses your space and prepares CAD designs and 2D/3D renderings for individual products or a complete room solution, so the layout is agreed before anything is manufactured.

How can I find pricing for FURNISYS products?

You can request a price quote by filling out our online form, contacting your local reseller, or calling our Customer Service Department at +91-704-576-3012.

Where can I read FURNISYS’s Terms and Conditions?

Click here to view our Terms and Conditions of Sale.

I found a product photo on your website that I want to order. How do I get pricing and place an order?

For detailed product and pricing information, please fill out a request-for-quote form or contact our Customer Service Department at +91-704-576-3012.

Products & finishes

What if I can’t find products that perfectly fit my requirements?

FURNISYS is committed to delivering Configured solutions tailored to your exact needs. Contact us to learn more.

I want to find specific FURNISYS products but can’t locate them on your website.

Our website provides an overview of our product offerings. To see our complete product line, visit our “Download” page to download product PDFs.

What color finishes do you offer? Can I request a specific colour?

Furnisys offers 5 standard powder-coat colours with a matt finish:

Light GreyRAL 7035Dark GreyRAL 7015BlackRAL 9005Gentian BlueRAL 5010RedRAL 3002
What locking and security systems do you offer?

CAM LOCK and 2 keys for the base cabinet, Hanging drawers & Mobile Cabinet.

How does FURNISYS help with 5S methodology implementation?

Our workspace and storage solutions support 5S by efficiently sorting and organizing items, streamlining manufacturing processes, and reducing overall costs.

How does FURNISYS help prevent ESD damage?

Electrostatic discharge can damage sensitive electronic components. FURNISYS offers ESD mat with accessories to create a static-safe workspace. Learn more about our ESD manufacturing solutions and specialized workstations.
