Flexi Workstations

Request a consultProduct literature

Adapt today, Configure for tomorrow

Flexi Workstations offer ultimate adaptability for both current and future needs. Maximize productivity per square foot with their flexibility. These industrial workstations can be seamlessly incorporated into a variety of setups, including in-line workstations, double-sided workstations, as well as configurations in U, X, L, and T shapes, optimizing workflow efficiency while remaining cost-effective.

Product Overview

Flexi system frame - Double sided Macro/Micro Slot

Advantage: Allows maximum accessory integration and enables a double-sided workstation.
Benefits: Maximizes functionality per square foot.

Variety of Worksurfaces (GI + Plyboard - GW)


Advantage: Offers a range of medium-duty worksurface options.
Benefits: Enhances work efficiency and durability.

Available Hanging Drawer

Advantage: Adds extra storage space below the worksurface.
Benefits: Enables lean and organized storage.

Variety of Shelves (Variable Angle Shelf)

Advantage: Adjustable positioning to suit individual user needs.
Benefits: Improves space utilization and ergonomic flexibility.

Variety of Panels (Pegboard, Privacy, Reversible Marker/Tack Boards)

Advantage: Sturdy panels easily attach between frame uprights.
Benefits: Enhances work efficiency and boosts productivity.

Variety of Rails (Heavy-Duty Parts Bin Rails, Power & Tool Rail)

Advantage: Supports storage and accessibility via bin, power, and tool rails.
Benefits: Provides a lean storage solution.

Lighting Provision (Switchable 4-Lamp Fluorescent Light Assembly)

Advantage: Easily adjustable light fixture for optimal positioning.
Benefits: Ensures a well-lit work area, reducing errors.

Starter/Adder Frame

Advantage: Enables easy reconfiguration and expansion to meet evolving needs.
Benefits: Optimizes both vertical and horizontal space.

Powder-Coated Finish (With Color Options)

Advantage: Protects against corrosion and abrasion.
Benefits: Increases product lifespan, resists discoloration, and enhances workspace aesthetics.
