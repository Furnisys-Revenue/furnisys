Furnisys sales course.

A step-by-step course for selling configured workspace systems, from finding the right organisations to closing the order and handing it over. Built for channel partners and sales teams.

Start the courseBecome a channel partner

Who it's for.

Channel partners

Dealers, consultants, integrators and referrers selling Furnisys systems in their region.

Sales and pre-sales teams

New team members learning to qualify, configure and propose.

Project teams

People who turn an order into a smooth installation and handover.

Seven modules, first contact to order.

Module 0 covers the system. Modules 1 to 6 follow the Furnisys sales workflow, in the order a deal moves.

0. Know the system1. Prospecting2. Pitch3. Lead analysis4. Price proposal5. Negotiation and review6. Order closure
Module 0

Know the system

Before you sell, know what you are selling.

  • Three programs: Industrial System, Wall System and Lockers System
  • Work systems (Base, Pro, Scitec, Link, Flexi), storage systems and accessories
  • Pre-configured products versus configurable solutions
  • Why Furnisys sells standard modules, never one-off builds
Use in this module:Product trainingSystem benefitsEbooks and catalogues
Module 1

Prospecting

Find the organisations and the people who buy workspace systems.

  • Industries with ready solutions: manufacturing, aerospace, automotive, oil and gas, healthcare, gems and jewellery, education, government
  • Who decides: plant heads, maintenance and quality managers, lab heads, project and purchase teams
  • Signals worth acting on: a new line, a lab set-up, an expansion, an audit finding
  • Focus on lines, labs and facilities, not one-off single-bench enquiries
Use in this module:IndustriesNewsletters to share with prospects
Module 2

Pitch

Lead with the customer's work, not the catalogue.

  • Open with the space and the problem: tool search, static damage, rework, kitting
  • Show a matching solution and a case study from the same industry
  • Explain the system advantage: reconfigure, extend and reuse
  • Use the industry brochure as the leave-behind
Use in this module:Solutions libraryCase studiesIndustry brochures
Module 3

Lead analysis

Understand the requirement before proposing anything.

  • Ask about the work done at each station, the parts and tools handled and the loads
  • Capture the space: layout drawing, photos, floor, power and earthing
  • Note ESD, cleanliness, chemical or washdown needs
  • Confirm quantity, timeline and who signs off
Use in this module:Site-readiness checklistUse cases
Module 4

Price proposal

Turn the requirement into a configuration and a quote.

  • Choose pre-configured products where they fit; configure where the application demands it
  • Build the configuration: work system, worktop, size, accessories, storage and colour
  • Read and write part numbers correctly
  • Share the layout with the quotation
Use in this module:How to configure a workstationPart numbers and worktop codes
Module 5

Negotiation and review

Agree pricing, terms and specifications, and defend the value.

  • Compare the lifecycle cost, not just the day-one price
  • Answer the fabricator, product seller and integrator comparisons with facts
  • Revise the configuration rather than discounting the system
  • Confirm specifications in writing before closure
Use in this module:Compare buying optionsFurnisys vs Metafold
Module 6

Order closure

Close the order and set up a smooth handover.

  • Receive the purchase order with shop drawing approval and advance
  • Share the site-readiness checklist with the customer
  • Agree the installation date and the handover pack
  • Introduce after-sales support so the customer knows where to go next
Use in this module:How to buyGetting startedSupport plans
