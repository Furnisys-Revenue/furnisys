Lockable Nine-Drawer Cabinets for an Industrial Pump Manufacturer

Three 57-inch base cabinets with nine drawers of mixed heights, heavy-duty telescopic slides rated 60 kg per drawer, integrated handles and central locking, replacing worn storage.

Storage that is used all day

Situation

The client manufactures industrial pumps and fluid handling systems. On their production floor, storage cabinets are opened constantly by operators and maintenance staff.

Worn slides and no locks

The challenge

Their existing cabinets had worn out. Ageing drawer slides made drawers hard to open, slowing access to tools and equipment. The client wanted the same footprint, drawer capacity and load rating, with better reliability and a lockable system for controlled access.

How do you upgrade storage without changing the floor plan?

The question

How do you replace worn cabinets with something smoother and more secure, without changing the space they occupy?

Three standalone base cabinets

The Furnisys approach

  • Height: 57 inches, matching the existing footprint
  • Drawers: nine per cabinet in 2", 3", 5" and 7" heights for tools, instruments, consumables and equipment of different sizes
  • Slides: heavy-duty telescopic slides, smoother and more durable than the old ball-bearing slides, rated up to 60 kg UDL per drawer
  • Handles: integrated handle design on every drawer
  • Security: central locking for controlled access

Sized drawers, smooth access, secure contents

How the setup works together

Shallow drawers hold small instruments and consumables, deeper drawers hold heavier tools. Telescopic slides keep heavy drawers easy to open, and central locking secures everything at once.

What the client received

What the configuration enabled

  • Smooth, reliable drawer operation under frequent use
  • Up to 60 kg per drawer for heavier tools
  • Secure, centrally locked storage
  • A like-for-like footprint with better usability

The slide is the cabinet

Furnisys perspective

A cabinet is only as good as its drawers' movement. Heavy-duty telescopic slides are what keep storage usable after years of daily use.

Frequently asked questions

What load can a Furnisys cabinet drawer carry?

In this configuration, heavy-duty telescopic slides supported up to 60 kg UDL per drawer.

Can drawer heights be mixed in one cabinet?

Yes. These cabinets combined 2-, 3-, 5- and 7-inch drawers, nine in total.

Do base cabinets lock?

Yes. These cabinets used central locking to secure all drawers at once.
