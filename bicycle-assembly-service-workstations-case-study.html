Assembly and Service Workstations for a Premium Bicycle Store

Pro Workstations with base cabinets and Link Uprighters for a store where bikes are assembled live in front of customers, plus a matching station for repairs.

Bikes built where customers can watch

Situation

The client sells premium bicycles, accessories and spare parts. In their store, bikes are assembled live and handed to the customer, and a separate area handles servicing. The benches had to work hard and look the part.

Overseas lead times, local quality gaps

The challenge

The client first looked at overseas vendors, but long lead times and no service support in India ruled them out. Local fabricators struggled to understand the requirement and delivered inconsistent quality. After further research the client came to Furnisys.

How do you build a showroom-grade workshop?

The question

How do you give technicians every tool they need for assembly and repair, keep grease and parts out of sight, and keep the space presentable in front of customers?

Two areas, one system

The Furnisys approach

1. Assembly area: Pro Workstations with base cabinets and Link Uprighter

Pegboards on the uprighter hold Allen keys, torque wrenches, spanners, screwdrivers, pliers, chain tools, pedal wrenches, tyre levers, brake and shifter tools, calipers, and cable cutters and crimpers. Base cabinets store gears, chains, brake pads, heavy tools and spare grease and oil.

2. Repair and maintenance: Pro Workstation with base cabinets and Link Uprighter

The service station holds screwdrivers, pliers, wrenches, levels, tape measures and utility knives on the uprighter, with replacement parts, repair kits and lubricants in the cabinets below.

Tools up, consumables down

How the setup works together

Every hand tool hangs at eye level on the pegboards; heavy and messy items live in the base cabinets. The worktop stays clear for the bike, which matters when customers are watching the build.

What the client received

What the configuration enabled

  • Assembly and service stations from one consistent system
  • Tools organised for quick, visible access
  • Enclosed storage for parts, lubricants and heavy tools
  • A workshop that suits a customer-facing store, with local service support

Workshops can be part of the brand

Furnisys perspective

In a store where assembly is part of the experience, the workstation is on display. A heavy-duty Pro frame and a tidy uprighter give both strength and presentation.

Frequently asked questions

What tools should a bicycle assembly workstation hold?

Allen keys, torque wrench, spanners, screwdrivers, pliers, chain tool, pedal wrench, tyre levers, brake and shifter tools, calipers and cable cutters.

Why use base cabinets under a bicycle workbench?

They keep grease, oils, spare parts and heavy tools enclosed and close at hand, so the worktop stays clear.

Can the same system serve assembly and repair?

Yes. Both areas here use Pro Workstations with base cabinets and Link Uprighters, with different tool sets.
