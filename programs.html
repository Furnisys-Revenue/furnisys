LISTA

Modular industrial workstations, workbenches and storage. For production lines, maintenance bays, testing labs, packaging areas and training centres.

Enquire about LISTA

LISTA configurations combine a leg structure, a worktop chosen for the task, storage, and vertical systems for tools and power. The same components repeat across stations, so a layout can be expanded or reorganised later.

  • Ansys Workbench, a workbench with integrated storage
  • Pro, for heavy-duty and impact work
  • Scitec, for precision, lab and process work, with ESD options

Wall Control

Wall-mounted tool and storage organisation for workshops, garages, woodworking and craft spaces, offices and utility areas.

Enquire about Wall Control

Wall Control uses metal pegboard panels and modular accessories to give every tool a defined place on the wall. Tools stay visible, return to the same spot and stay off the work surface.

How it works

Panels mount to the wall and accept hooks, bins, shelves and holders. Accessories move as needs change, so the layout can be rearranged without new panels.

Why metal panels

Metal panels carry heavier tools than basic boards and keep their shape in daily workshop use.

Where it is used

  • Garages and tool areas
  • Garden sheds and woodworking spaces
  • Craft and hobby rooms
  • Home offices, kitchens, laundry and closets

Scaling up

Start with one wall or one workstation, then repeat the same panels and accessory set across other stations or areas.

Lockers

Lockers Manufacturing. Steel lockers for sports facilities, schools and colleges, workplaces, healthcare, fire and police, and military and government.

Enquire about Lockers

Lockers are made to order in two construction series. Choose the series for the level of use, then the tiers, ventilation, handles and locks for the site.

Knock Down series

Prime mild CR steel with rivet and bolt assembly. 16G door, door frame and base; 24G top, back, sides and shelf. Standard louver ventilation. Easy to transport, assemble, dismantle and reconfigure.

All Welded series

Welded into one rigid unit and shipped fully assembled. 14G door with reinforcement; 16G top, sides, base, frame and shelf; 18G back. Diamond perforation ventilation as standard. Built for heavy use.

Standard features

  • 2 mil epoxy/polyester powder coat, baked to cure
  • Recessed stainless handle that accepts a padlock or built-in lock
  • 16G full-length continuous piano hinge
  • Continuous door strike with rubber bumpers
  • Polished aluminium number plates
  • Interior shelf, coat rod and hooks, by locker size

Options

  • 1 to 6 tiers and 2-person duplex (Knock Down); 1 to 9 tiers (Welded)
  • Diamond or rectangular perforations, louvers, or solid
  • Multi-point or single-point recessed latch, or door pull with padlock hasp
  • Padlock, cam key lock or built-in combination lock with master key
  • Slope tops, closed and zee bases, fillers
