Programs and products

Each program addresses a specific need. Knowing which program fits keeps scope clear and outcomes predictable.

Industrial System

Modular industrial workstations, workbenches and storage for production lines, maintenance bays, testing labs, packaging areas and training centres.

Enquire about Industrial System

Industrial System configurations combine a leg structure, a worktop chosen for the task, storage, and vertical systems for tools and power. The same components repeat across stations, so a layout can be expanded or reorganised later.

Workbenches

  • Base Workbench, the simpler bench for general and training use
  • Ansys Workbench, with integrated storage
  • Pro, for heavy-duty and impact work
  • Scitec, for precision, lab and process work, with ESD options

Link system

  • Link Uprighter (starter and adder)
  • Link Workstand
  • Link Trolley
  • Link Flow Racks

Flexi system

  • In-line, back-to-back and C-shaped layouts
  • Universal carts
  • Flow racks

Worktops

  • Postform laminate and ESD laminate
  • GI cladding, and GI with wood
  • Stainless steel
  • High-pressure laminate (HPL)

Accessories

  • Pegboards and solid boards
  • Horizontal power rails
  • Instrument riser and adjustable shelves
  • Task lights, monitor arms, footrests, bench vises, castors

Wall System

Wall-mounted tool and storage organisation for workshops, garages, woodworking and craft spaces, offices and utility areas.

Enquire about Wall System

Wall System uses metal pegboard panels and modular accessories to give every tool a defined place on the wall. Tools stay visible, return to the same spot and stay off the work surface.

How it works

Panels mount to the wall and accept hooks, bins, shelves and holders. Accessories move as needs change, without new panels.

Why metal panels

Metal panels carry heavier tools than basic boards and keep their shape in daily workshop use.

Where it is used

  • Garages and tool areas
  • Garden sheds and woodworking spaces
  • Craft and hobby rooms
  • Home offices, kitchens, laundry and closets

Scaling up

Start with one wall or workstation, then repeat the same panels and accessories across other areas.

Lockers System

Steel lockers for sports facilities, schools and colleges, workplaces, healthcare, fire and police, and military and government.

Explore Lockers System

Lockers are configured from two construction series. Choose the series for the level of use, then the tiers, ventilation, handles and locks for the site.

Knock Down series

Prime mild CR steel with rivet and bolt assembly. 16G door, door frame and base; 24G top, back, sides and shelf. Standard louver ventilation. Easy to transport, assemble, dismantle and reconfigure.

All Welded series

Welded into one rigid unit and shipped fully assembled. 14G door with reinforcement; 16G top, sides, base, frame and shelf; 18G back. Diamond perforation ventilation as standard.

Standard features

  • 2 mil epoxy/polyester powder coat, baked to cure
  • Recessed stainless handle, padlock or built-in lock
  • 16G full-length continuous piano hinge
  • Door strike with rubber bumpers
  • Polished aluminium number plates
  • Interior shelf, coat rod and hooks, by size

Options

  • 1–6 tiers and duplex (Knock Down); 1–9 tiers (Welded)
  • Perforated, louvered or solid doors
  • Multi-point or single-point latch, or padlock hasp
  • Padlock, cam key or combination lock with master key
  • Slope tops, closed and zee bases, fillers
