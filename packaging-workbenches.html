Optimizing Packaging Workbenches (White Paper)

Tailored Configurations for Industry-Specific Needs

Packaging affects efficiency, product safety and brand perception; standardised workstations cause inefficiency, higher labour cost and ergonomic problems. E-commerce: modular benches with barcode scanners, packing material dispensers, integrated scales. Pharma: stainless steel surfaces, anti-static materials, contamination-free zones. Automotive/aerospace: reinforced structures, heavy load, bulk zones. Adjustable benches, anti-fatigue mats, height adjustment improve comfort.

Key Considerations for Designing Industry-Specific Packaging Workbenches

Understand environment, tasks, ergonomic standards, materials and regulations.

Layout Considerations in the Packaging Area

  • Placement should follow material flow to avoid bottlenecks.
  • Beside conveyors: smooth transfer; tasks include assembly, boxing, weighing, labelling.
  • Without conveyors: linear or U-shaped layouts for one-way flow; packing islands for high volume.
  • Example: automotive components unit places a bench at the end of a conveyor to receive, inspect, label and dispatch.

Ergonomics & Worker Comfort

Furnisys applies recognised standards and anthropometric data.

Ergonomic Workstation Design: Height and Reach Zones

Based on average Indian heights (165 cm male, 152 cm female):

  • Seated work (labeling, document handling): 750–850 mm
  • Standing work (carton sealing, weighing): 900–1100 mm
  • Primary reach zone (450–500 mm): tape dispensers, cutters, printers
  • Secondary reach zone (up to 1000 mm): spare labels, packaging rolls

Material & Surface Selection Based on Industry Requirements

Furnisys makes mild steel (MS) furniture; worktop choice affects chemical resistance, load and longevity.

| Worktop Type | Application | Suitability | Key Benefits |

|---|---|---|---|

| PLW | General packaging and assembly | Cost-effective | Smooth surface for standard tasks |

| PELW | Electronics | Electronics packaging | Scratch-resistant, easy to clean, durable |

| HPL | Laboratory packaging, chemical-resistant areas | Chemical-resistant areas | Chemical resistance, smooth surface for precision tasks |

| SS Cladded | Pharmaceuticals, food packaging | Hygienic packaging | Corrosion-resistant, hygienic, easy to sanitize |

| GI Cladded | Heavy-duty industrial packaging | Heavy-duty use | Impact resistant, high durability |

Storage & Organization for Efficient Workflow

Key Storage Accessories for Packaging Workbenches

  • Under Worktop Storage: bottom shelves with vertical solid dividers; hanging drawers for tools and QC checklists.
  • Task-Specific Holders: roll holders for thin film (electronics, medical devices).

Task-Specific Workbench Considerations

  • Boxing and wrapping: large flat stable surface, adjustable carton shelving, roll holders (e-commerce, retail).
  • Labeling and printing: label dispensers, document holders, printer shelves, space for barcode scanners and QC docs (medical devices, food).
  • Weighing and quality control: pharma, industrial manufacturing, logistics.
  • Pre-packing assembly: tool rails, power outlets, component bins, bin rails, hanging drawers.

Safety & Compliance – Industry-Specific Guidelines

  • OSHA ergonomic guidelines
  • ISO 14644 (cleanroom standards for pharmaceuticals and electronics)
  • GMP (food and pharma)
  • IS 12433 (Indian standard for industrial safety and ergonomics)

Industry-Specific Packaging Workbench Configurations

Heavy-Duty Packaging for Bearings

Scitec Workbench with GI + Wood Cladding worktop; weighing machine and automatic sealing machine; under-worktop shelves with dividers; Link Uprighter with solid board and whiteboard; adjustable shelf; fixed tube light; horizontal power rail; monitor arm.

Tea Packaging & Billing Workstation

PLW worktop with weighing scale, thermal printer, barcode scanner; tray assemblies; utility bin; adjustable shelf; vertical power rail; monitor arm with keyboard tray; overhead light.

Valve Manufacturer's Packaging & Final Inspection Stations

Packaging stations: three Base Workbenches with Link Uprighters, pack shelves, roll holders; valves cleaned first (nuclear, specialised industrial, instrumentation valves). Final station: Base Workbench with Link Uprighter, barcode scanners, label printer, computer, weighing scale; traceability for Digital Services and Hydrogen Valves.

Cosmetic Product Packaging & Labeling

Link Workstand with PLW worktop, two adjustable shelves, monitor arm, keyboard tray; roll holders mounted internally and externally; Link System's low load capacity suits lightweight products and lowers cost.

Packaging Workbench Beside a Conveyor – Electronics Industry

PELW worktop; labelling, barcode scanning, QC; monitor arm and keyboard tray; two adjustable shelves; roll holder; packed items go directly onto conveyor.

Reducing Fatigue and Enhancing Workflow

  • Adjustable shelving and bin rails
  • Footrests and anti-fatigue mats
  • Overhead and task lighting
  • Optimized reach zones

The Role of Modular & Scalable Workstations in Future-Proofing Packaging

Adaptability & Integration with the Link System

Link Uprighter: heavy-gauge MS frame fitting Base, Pro, Scitec and Pack Workbenches; retrofittable. Accessories: tool rails, document holders, bin rails, pegboards. Link Workstand: standalone, single- or double-sided.

Scalability & Configurability with the Flexi System

  • Layouts: back-to-back, inline, T, X
  • Macro/Micro Slot System: macro slots in 3" increments, micro slots in 1" increments
  • Starter/Adder Upright Assembly: add sections incrementally
  • Optimized reach zones

Future-Proofing Workspaces with the Right Workbench

  • Base Series: 454 kg UDL — general packaging
  • Pro Series: up to 1000 kg UDL — heavy-duty
  • Scitec Series: ESD-compatible — static-sensitive electronics packaging
  • Accessories: roll holders, shelf dividers, adjustable shelving

Conclusion: Maximizing Packaging Efficiency with the Right Workbench

Efficiency depends on ergonomics, modularity and scalability; Link and Flexi systems adapt with minimal disruption.
