Furnisys vs Metafold.

Metafold is an example of the fabricator route: welded furniture made to a drawing for one site. Here is how it compares with a configured Furnisys system, so you can choose what fits your project.

Discuss your projectCompare all options

About Metafold

Metafold Engineering Pvt. Ltd., Pune, Maharashtra

Lists industrial storage cabinets, locker cabinets, cupboards, electronic workbenches, assembly tables, racking and structure fabrication.

Source: IndiaMART listing, as published in November 2026.

About Furnisys

Furnisys, a brand of Fabtech India, Navi Mumbai

Modular, configurable work and storage systems (Industrial System, Wall System and Lockers System), designed, manufactured and installed from our 22,000 sq. ft. facility since 2018.

When Metafold may suit you

  • You need a one-off item that no standard range covers
  • The layout will never change
  • The lowest day-one price matters more than anything after it

What to watch for

  • Changing the layout means rework or new fabrication
  • Storage, accessories and benches rarely match
  • Quality and finish vary between batches and vendors
  • Little or no documentation, spares or after-sales

How a Furnisys system differs

  • Knock-down modules that are reconfigured, not rebuilt
  • Benches, storage and accessories designed as one system
  • Consistent build from our own 22,000 sq. ft. facility
  • Manuals, technical docs and standard spare parts
