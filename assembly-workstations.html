Assembly workstations, ready to configure.

An assembly workstation is a workbench built out for one assembly task: the right frame for the load, a worktop for the process, and shelves, bins, pegboard, light and power placed in the order the work happens. Furnisys offers 14 ready configurations, from Base Series stations rated 454 kg to Pro Series benches rated 850 to 1,000 kg, ESD-safe Scitec benches and Flexi lines that add stations as output grows. All are designed and made in Navi Mumbai.

A station that grows with the line.

Every configuration is built from standard modules, so it can be extended, rearranged or moved to another line instead of replaced.

  • Add a riser shelf, then a hanging drawer and rear riser, then a pegboard and overhead cabinet as the task grows.
  • Flexi lines take any number of adder and corner units, so a line extends without replacing the first stations.
  • Link Uprighters mount on Base, Pro, Scitec or an existing workbench (one starter plus one adder per run).
  • Base cabinets slide under Pro and Scitec benches later; mobile cabinets work beside any bench.

Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between an assembly workbench and an assembly workstation?

An assembly workbench is the frame and worktop. An assembly workstation adds what the task needs at the bench: uprighter, shelves, bins, pegboard, light, power and storage, placed in the order the work happens.

Which Furnisys workbench suits heavy assembly?

The Pro Series. Its welded legs carry 850 to 1,000 kg evenly distributed, for heavy parts, pressing, hammering and disassembly. Base and Scitec benches carry 360 to 454 kg.

Can an assembly line be extended later?

Yes. Flexi lines take any number of adder and corner units, so stations are added without replacing the first ones. Link Uprighters and Link Workstands extend by one starter plus one adder per run.

Do you make ESD-safe assembly workstations?

Yes. Scitec benches take an ESD laminate worktop (PELW) with a wrist strap and ground kit, and Flexi lines are used in ESD-safe electronics labs.

Are these configurations ready-made or made to order?

Base Series and the storage range are a pre-configured range. Pro, Scitec, Link and Flexi are configurable: the configurations shown here are starting points that our team adapts to your task and layout.

Where are Furnisys workstations made?

In Furnisys's 22,000 sq. ft. facility in Navi Mumbai, covering cutting, CNC bending, welding, pre-treatment, powder coating, assembly and inspection, with installation across India.
