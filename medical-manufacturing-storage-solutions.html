Clean Rooms

Keep your medical device clean room running smoothly with Furnisys industrial furniture. Our range includes durable mild steel workbenches, and storage cabinets all essential for maintaining the pristine conditions necessary for producing medical devices and pharmaceuticals. Plus, our custom design options ensure a perfect fit for your space and specific needs, maximizing efficiency and productivity.

Esd Products

Protecting sensitive components from electrostatic discharge starts with grounding the individuals who handle them forming the baseline of an effective, static-safe workspace. Furnisys offers a complete lineup of ESD-safe solutions, including configurable Flexi and Scitec workstations, storage cabinets with grounding capabilities, and static-dissipative worktops such as PELW or ESD mats. To support a fully static-safe environment, we also provide ESD accessories like wrist straps, cords, and grounding…

Medical Assembly

Meet the unique requirements of your medical device assembly facility with our comprehensive solutions. Our offerings include versatile assembly workstations, ensuring streamlined operations. Protect sensitive electronics with ESD shielding. Additionally, optimize your machining areas with our tool storage and transport systems.

Medical Tool Storage

From stationary cabinets to trolleys to workbenches, Furnisys offers sturdy and safe storage solutions for tools used in medical manufacturing. Our products come with a modular design, drawers that can be divided into sections, and durable tool holders. This allows you to easily arrange and keep track of tools, ensuring they remain in good condition and ready for use.

Medical Quality Testing & Inspection

Furnisys industrial furniture is the foundation of your medical device testing and inspection operations. Our technical furniture and storage cabinets are designed to optimize space, making storage more efficient and improving organization. Choose us to streamline your operations and enhance productivity in your medical manufacturing facility.
