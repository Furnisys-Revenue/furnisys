A Two-Technician Assembly Workstation for a Factory Moving to Automation

A dual-user Scitec Workstation with a Link Uprighter, a Base Workbench for preparation work and a mobile cabinet, configured for a cosmetics packaging plant's tool room.

A shopfloor being rebuilt around automation

Situation

The client was upgrading sections of their factory with automation-driven systems. Their engineering and maintenance team needed a proper place in the tool room to assemble the mechanical and structural parts that would go into the new setup: conveyor sections, motorised actuators, sensor mounts, filling and dosing units and light structural frames.

Two technicians, one bench, and parts that keep changing

The challenge

The brief was specific. One assembly table for two technicians working at the same time, rated for 150 kg, sized 914 mm (H) × 1524 mm (L) × 762 mm (D). It needed a pegboard with drawers, bin strips, holders for screwdrivers, spanners and Allen keys, and an adjustable shelf with an undershelf task light.

The team also wanted a second bench for preparatory work and mobile tool storage, so technicians would not be tied to one spot while setups moved across the floor.

How do you equip a tool room for assemblies that don't exist yet?

The question

How do you build an assembly zone that suits whatever the automation project needs next, without reconfiguring the room every time the parts change?

A flexible core with support around it

The Furnisys approach

1. Scitec Workstation with Link Uprighter

The centre of the solution is a Scitec Workstation for two users, with a postform laminated worktop rated for 150 kg and a hanging drawer unit for hand tools and jigs. A Link Uprighter above the worktop carries:

  • A pegboard with medium slotted hooks for tools and fixtures that rotate with the job
  • A bin strip for fasteners, washers and connectors
  • Screwdriver holder, spanner tray and Allen key holder
  • An adjustable shelf with an undershelf task light for precise alignment work

2. Base Workbench for preparation

A Base Workbench with a postform laminated top acts as the staging surface: unpacking kits, inspecting parts, pre-wiring and sub-assembly before work moves to the main station.

3. Mobile Cabinet

A six-drawer mobile cabinet carries hand tools, precision instruments and fastener assortments to wherever the work is.

Prepare, assemble, move

How the setup works together

Parts are staged and checked on the Base Workbench, assembled at the Scitec Workstation, and the mobile cabinet follows the technicians across the shopfloor during installation and fine-tuning. Nobody walks back to a fixed store for a tool.

What the client received

What the configuration enabled

  • A dual-user assembly station rated for 150 kg with tools, bins and light at hand
  • A separate surface for preparatory and secondary tasks
  • Mobile tool storage that keeps pace with a changing shopfloor
  • Accessories that can be moved or added as assembly needs change

Plan for change, not for one job

Furnisys perspective

Automation projects rarely end where they start. A slot-based uprighter lets the team rearrange accessories for brackets today and sensor enclosures tomorrow, without replacing the bench.

Frequently asked questions

What size assembly table suits two technicians?

This project used a table 914 mm high, 1524 mm long and 762 mm deep with a 150 kg rating, configured as a Scitec Workstation with a Link Uprighter.

Why add a separate Base Workbench?

It gives technicians a staging surface for unpacking, inspection and pre-wiring, keeping the main assembly station clear.

Can accessories be changed later?

Yes. Accessories on the Link Uprighter mount in slots and can be added, removed or repositioned as the work changes.
