Optimized Storage & Workspaces for Manufacturing Efficiency

In high-performance manufacturing environments, precision, safety, and efficiency are non-negotiable. Whether you're operating an assembly line, a fabrication cell, or a quality control lab, organized and ergonomic workspaces can make all the difference. Furnisys modular storage and workbench solutions are engineered to streamline workflows, reduce downtime, and support lean manufacturing practices. By minimizing risks such as Foreign Object Debris (FOD), cross-contamination, electrostatic discharge (ESD), and ergonomic strain, our systems ensure cleaner, safer, and more productive work environments. From sturdy workstations and accessible storage to mobile units and ESD-safe configurations, we help manufacturers maintain operational excellence across every function.

Electronic Assembly

Furnisys workstations are engineered to support precise and efficient electronic assembly tasks, whether it involves circuit boards, small parts, or full box builds. Designed with modular flexibility, they adapt easily to evolving processes and workflows.

Our solutions incorporate ESD-safe materials and accessories to protect sensitive components while maintaining an ergonomic setup that reduces fatigue and boosts accuracy during extended assembly operations.

LAN/Computers

Furnisys Flexi Workstations offer the adaptability needed for LAN setups, testing environments, programming stations, and computer-based classrooms. Designed for evolving tech spaces, these modular systems provide a structured and ergonomic setup that organizes both hardware and tools efficiently.

Whether you're managing servers, performing diagnostics, or configuring systems, the Flexi System allows you to create efficient, clutter-free work areas

Progressive Assembly

In progressive assembly environments, modular workstations serve as essential support structures alongside conveyor systems. They create defined zones for each stage of the build process, maintaining workflow continuity and organization.

With options for integrated storage, and ESD protection, these workstations help reduce operator fatigue and improve precision. Ideal for sub-assembly tasks or build sequences where reliable support and workflow adaptability are key.

Packing, Shipping and Receiving

Engineered with advanced features, our packaging workstations streamline your operations for seamless handling. Enhance any Furnisys workbench solution with our array of fully customizable accessories, ensuring every packing and shipping requirement is met both above and below the worksurface.

Parts Storage and Kitting

Designed to streamline computer networking, testing, programming, repair, classroom activities, and analytical tasks, our modular system ensures a cost-effective and efficient setup tailored to your needs.

With ample working space and ergonomic optimization, our workstations minimize strain, fatigue, and workplace injuries, empowering your team to excel.

Work Cells

For cellular manufacturing in the electronic industry, ergonomic industrial workbenches with adjustable heights and modular configurations are ideal. Furnisys workstations feature ESD (electrostatic discharge) protection to safeguard delicate electronic components. Additionally, sturdy shelving units and tool storage solutions optimize efficiency and organization within the production cell.

Workstations for R&D

Maximize efficiency, organization, and safety in your R&D department with Furnisys workspace systems. Our range includes productivity-boosting workbenches and high-density modular drawer storage cabinets, ensuring you have everything you need to excel. Explore our extensive selection of ESD accessories prioritizing safety as you work diligently.

Workstation for Testing & Calibration

Enhance efficiency, organization, and safety in your electronic industry's test and calibration department with Furnisys workspace systems. From securing testing equipment safely to optimizing workflow, our solutions cater to your specific needs. Explore our range of productivity-enhancing workbenches and high-density modular drawer storage cabinets. Ensure safety with our extensive selection of static dissipative, products and accessories.

Repairing industrial workstations

For an Electronics Repair workspace, sturdy and ergonomic industrial workbenches equipped with adjustable shelves and drawers for organizing tools and components are essential.
Discover how our modular design, adaptable standard parts, ESD protection, and wide range of accessories can boost productivity and efficiency in your workspace.
