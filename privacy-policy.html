Privacy Policy

(Introduction)

Fabtech India ("Furnisys"/"We") describes what it collects from users of www.furnisys.co; read with Terms of Use (linked as furnisys.co/terms-and-condtition). Covers Personally Identifiable Information collected at registration, access or any action on the site.

What personal information do we collect from the people that visit our website or blog?

  • At registration: name, email, phone or other details.
  • Any information entered or provided ("Personal Information").
  • Third-party sign-in (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Pinterest, LinkedIn): username, encrypted access credentials, name, profile picture, country, hometown, email, date of birth, gender, friends' names, networks.
  • Contacts not imported without explicit permission; if permitted, used only to find friends using Furnisys.
  • Device data: hardware model, OS version, unique device identifiers, mobile network info incl. phone number.
  • Usage data: approximate location from IP, crash/system activity, hardware settings, browser type/language, request date/time, referral URLs.
  • Interaction data from emails, SMS, in-app messages (push notifications, interstitials).

When do we collectinformation?

Registration, product enquiries, content submission, discussion boards/social features, promotions/surveys, problem reports; also from affiliates, business partners, third parties (e.g., partner click-through data, social media account info).

How do we use your information?

Personalise content/offers; improve the site; contests, promotions, surveys; registration/account management; communicate about Furnisys and product specifications; publish reviews/posts; process enquiries; measure interest; send offers per marketing preferences; customise experience, resolve disputes, troubleshoot; prevent prohibited activity, enforce Terms.

Furnisys may share your information with the following entities:

  • Third-party vendors (analytics, customer service, marketing, PR, surveys, fraud prevention, ad delivery)
  • Business partners offering joint products/services
  • Referring websites (name, email, mailing address, phone, preferences)
  • Corporate family: FabTech India Group, parents, domestic and international affiliates

Also legal process, defence of rights, investigations, safety, corporate transactions (merger, asset sale, bankruptcy).

How do we protect your information

Physical, administrative, technical and organisational safeguards; secured networks; limited staff access; SSL encryption for sensitive/credit info; firewalls and intrusion detection; payments via gateway provider, not stored on Furnisys servers.

Do we use 'cookies'?

Remember preferences; recognise returning visitors ("keep me signed in" passwords encrypted); customise content and advertising; measure features/ads/emails; aggregate traffic data. Users can block cookies via browser settings.

If users disable cookies in their browser:

Some features may not work.

Third-party disclosure

States it does not sell, trade or transfer PII to outside parties, and does not include third-party products/services on the site.

Google

Uses Google AdSense; Google uses cookies incl. the DART cookie; opt out via Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy; link to https://support.google.com/adwordspolicy/answer/1316548?hl=en. Partners/ad networks may also use cookies for tailored ads.

Demographics and Interests Reporting

First-party (Google Analytics) and third-party (DoubleClick) cookies used to compile ad impression/interaction data.

Opting out:

Google Ad Settings page; Network Advertising Initiative Opt Out page; Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Does our site allow third-party behavioral tracking?

States it does not.

Retention and Deletion of Data:

  • Deletes data except legally required transaction records (name, contact details, phone, transaction history).
  • Does not retain online banking PINs, TANs, passwords or card info.
  • Will never ask for personal/security/financial details by email; forward such requests to support@furnisys.co.
  • Report phishing to support@furnisys.co.
  • Unsubscribe by writing to support@furnisys.co.

Changes to Privacy Policy

Posted on site; material changes notified by email or site notice.
