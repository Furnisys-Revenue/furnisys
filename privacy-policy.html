Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fabtech India, which sells under the brand Furnisys ("Furnisys", "we", "us"), collects and uses personal data when you visit www.furnisys.co.in (the "Website") or contact us through it. It should be read with our Terms of Use.

1. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: when you send an enquiry, apply to become a partner, ask to be matched with a partner, raise a support request, or request a catalogue or brochure, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, company, city, state, and the details you share about your requirement (industry, products needed, quantity, timeline, order number, messages).
  • Chat and messages: if you use live chat on the Website, or contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive what you send and your contact details.
  • How you found us: the first page you visited, the website that referred you, and campaign tags in the link you clicked (such as UTM parameters or an advertising click ID). These are stored in your browser and sent with your enquiry.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and similar technical data, collected through our chat and analytics tools.

We do not collect payment card details on the Website, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, configurations and drawings, and follow up on your project.
  • To connect you with a Furnisys channel partner who serves your region, where you ask us to or where it helps us serve you.
  • To deliver catalogues, brochures and after-sales support you request.
  • To evaluate partner applications.
  • To send information about our products and services that is relevant to your enquiry. You can opt out at any time.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns bring enquiries, and to improve the Website.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations and to protect our rights.

3. Legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a form or contact us, and for legitimate uses permitted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, such as responding to a request you made. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us; this does not affect processing already carried out.

4. Who we share it with

  • Service providers that process data for us: Zoho (CRM, forms, live chat, analytics, email), Vercel (website hosting), and, where used, other analytics and advertising providers.
  • Channel partners of Furnisys, when your enquiry is passed to the partner who serves your region.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell your personal data. Some providers store data outside India; we use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The Website uses cookies and browser storage to keep it working, to remember which campaign or page brought you to us, to run live chat, and to measure visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features, such as chat, may then not work.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer records for as long as needed to respond to you, serve you as a customer, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Data we no longer need is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate another person to exercise your rights, as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to the Grievance Officer below. We will respond within the time required by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls, to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. Links to other websites

The Website links to sites we do not control, such as YouTube, LinkedIn and brochure viewers. Their privacy policies apply when you use them.

10. Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer, Fabtech India (Furnisys), EL-21, TTC Industrial Area, MIDC, Mahape, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra 400710. Email: enquiry@furnisys.co (subject line "Privacy"). Phone: +91 70457 63012.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
