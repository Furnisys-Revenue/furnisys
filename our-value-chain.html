Our value chain.

At Furnisys every function works from the same system. Product defines it, development engineers it, revenue connects it to the right application and operations delivers it, consistently, order after order.

Discuss your projectManufacturing infrastructure

Your workspace journey with Furnisys.

  1. 01

    Product & program

    Defines and governs the systems: what we build, why it exists and how it scales.

    • Application-based solutions
    • Modular systems
    • Quality assurance
  2. 02

    Development

    Designs and develops the system end to end, from engineering and space planning to manufacturing process and tooling, so every solution is well designed and reliably built.

    • System engineering
    • Space planning
    • Tooling development
  3. 03

    Revenue

    Connects customers with the right solutions by understanding their applications, explaining the options and delivering the most suitable product through direct sales and channel partners.

    • Customer solutions
    • Product guidance
    • Channel partnerships
  4. 04

    Operations

    Turns confirmed orders into delivered products through disciplined planning, quality assurance and timely dispatch, with consistency built into every cycle.

    • Order planning
    • Quality assurance
    • Timely dispatch
