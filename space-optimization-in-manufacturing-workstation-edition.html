Space Optimization in Manufacturing (Workstation Edition)

Cluttered workspaces cost time, productivity and safety; tips for tidy, efficient industrial workstations.

Streamline Your Space: The Power of 5S

  • Sort (Seiri) — discard outdated/unused/broken items; donate/recycle
  • Set In Order (Seiton) — labels, bins, tool holders, shelves; frequent items within arm's reach
  • Shine (Seiso) — wipe surfaces, sweep, empty bins
  • Standardize (Seiketsu) — label formats, colour-coded bins, designated tool areas
  • Sustain (Shitsuke) — regular reviews, teamwork, accountability

Organized Workspace: Your Key to Efficiency

  • Vertical Storage · Mobile Carts · Shadow Boards · Labeling is King · Lighting Matters

The Benefits of a Streamlined Space

  • Increased Productivity · Improved Safety · Enhanced Morale · Reduced Waste

Conclusion

organisation is an ongoing culture.
