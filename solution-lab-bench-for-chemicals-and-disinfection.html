Home / Resources / Solutions library Laboratories and wet process areas

Set up a lab bench that handles chemicals and daily disinfection

Worktops, sinks and storage chosen for wet, chemical work.

Industrial System
Request this setupAll solutions

The problem

Office-grade tables stain, swell and corrode under reagents and repeated cleaning. Drainage is improvised and accreditation audits keep raising the same findings.

Why it happens

The furniture was not specified for chemical exposure, wet work or daily disinfection.

How the setup comes together

  1. Choose the worktop by hazard: HPL for general lab work, SS for heavy washdown.
  2. Place sink cabinets beside the wet-work benches.
  3. Store reagents below the worktop and keep the work surface clear.
  4. Run power along the rail, away from water.

What changes

Surfaces hold up to chemicals and cleaning, wet and dry work stay separate and the lab is ready for inspection.

The setup

  • Specialty workbenchChemical-resistant configuration for analysis and wet processes
  • Sink cabinetSS or GI sink with chemical-resistant interior
  • Base storage cabinetReagent and consumable storage under an HPL or SS worktop
  • Link uprighter, shelves and overhead cabinetsShelving and power rail above the bench

All standard modules. Sizes, worktops and colours are chosen for your space.

HealthcareEducation & trainingOil & gasGems & jewellery

Standards it supports

ISO 15189 / NABL; NABH; Pollution Control Board norms for plating.
