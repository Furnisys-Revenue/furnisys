Quick tips

Step-by-step videos of about three minutes each. Set up, load, fit and look after your workstations the right way, from the first day of installation.

Most shop floors aren't flat. Place the bench, level it both ways on its glides, then run a quick pre-use check.

Know the rating of the bench and every accessory, spread heavy loads or put them over a leg frame or cabinet, and keep heavy items low.

Get boxes off the worktop: understand starter and adder frames, plan accessories by reach, and hook each one into the same notch on both frames.

A two-minute routine for every shift: test your wrist strap, follow the grounding path, and keep the mat clean and clear.

Sort, set in order with partitions and label every compartment, then shine, standardise and sustain with a quick check each shift.

Load at the back and pick at the front, keep one part number per lane, and stay within the ratings so gravity rotates your stock.
