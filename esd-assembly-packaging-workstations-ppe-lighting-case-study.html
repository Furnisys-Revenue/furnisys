ESD-Safe Assembly and Packaging Stations for PPE Lighting

Two Link Workstand configurations, one ESD-safe for soldering LED PCBs into protective housings and one for packing finished lights, replicating a layout the client already used in Europe.

A European process, set up in India

Situation

The client manufactures personal protective equipment, including helmets, respirators, protective clothing and safety shoes. For a new line producing PPE lights, they wanted the same ergonomic setup they used at their European facility, and shared reference images and a workstation drawing to show it.

Two stations with different priorities

The challenge

Assembly: soldering LED PCBs and fitting them into protective casings with a soldering gun, air gun and electric screwdriver. Load rating 50–100 kg. It had to be ESD-safe, with a monitor arm, lighting, power rail, pegboard, louver board and adjustable shelves.

Packaging: bubble-wrapping finished lights and boxing them, with tapes, foam inserts and cutters organised on the station and shelving above and below the worktop for consumables and finished goods.

How do you match a reference layout with local, standard systems?

The question

How do you reproduce a proven overseas workstation design from standard modules, while keeping static control tight on the assembly side and material flow simple on the packing side?

Same base system, two configurations

The Furnisys approach

1. ESD assembly workstation

A Link Workstand with a postform laminated worktop, an ESD mat and grounding blocks for complete static control. Accessories:

  • Horizontal power rail for soldering tools, air guns and electric screwdrivers
  • Pegboard with bin strip, slotted hooks and screwdriver holder
  • Adjustable shelf for PCBs, test equipment and in-process assemblies
  • Monitor arm for assembly reference and test visuals
  • Light fixture for soldering delicate LED contacts
  • Tool rail for light hand tools

2. Packaging workstation

A Link Workstand with a postform laminated top for bubble wrapping and handling finished lights, plus:

  • Pegboard with bin strip and hooks for tape dispensers, cutters and markers
  • A shelf with dividers above the worktop for bubble wrap and tape rolls
  • A shelf below for flat-packed cartons and finished items
  • An extra adjustable shelf for flexible storage

From solder joint to sealed box

How the setup works together

Lights are soldered and fitted into housings on the ESD station, then move to the packaging station where every consumable is within arm's reach. Because both stations use the same Link system, accessories can move between them if the line changes.

What the client received

What the configuration enabled

  • Static-safe handling of LED circuitry during assembly
  • A packing station with consumables and finished goods organised above and below the worktop
  • A setup matching the client's European reference, built from standard modules
  • Installation and after-sales support from Furnisys

Reference images are a starting point

Furnisys perspective

When a client brings a reference from another facility, we map each function to standard modules rather than copying the drawing line for line. The result works the same way and stays expandable.

Frequently asked questions

What makes an assembly workstation ESD-safe?

An ESD mat on the worktop, grounding blocks and wrist straps that safely dissipate static, so LED circuits and PCBs are not damaged during handling.

Can the same system be used for assembly and packaging?

Yes. Both stations here are Link Workstands with different accessories, so the line uses one system throughout.

Can Furnisys work from a client's reference images?

Yes. In this project the client's European reference images and drawing were translated into standard Furnisys configurations.
