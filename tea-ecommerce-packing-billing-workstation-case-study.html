A Packing and Billing Workstation for a Premium Tea Retailer

A Scitec workbench with Link Uprighter and pack accessories, then a Flexi Workstation chosen after a factory visit, set up for weighing, labelling, billing and packing tea orders.

Online orders that need packing and billing in one place

Situation

The client sells premium teas and teaware. Their dispatch team weighs, labels, bills and packs every order, often under peak-season pressure.

A defined footprint and many devices

The challenge

The client shared reference images and a size of 1500 × 900 × 1750 mm (L × W × H). The station had to hold a weighing scale, thermal printer, barcode scanner and computer, plus tools and packing consumables, without cluttering the worktop.

How do you fit a dispatch desk into one workstation?

The question

How do you give one operator everything needed to weigh, label, bill and pack an order, within a fixed footprint?

Two options, one decision at our factory

The Furnisys approach

Option 1: Scitec Workbench with Link Uprighter and pack accessories

Hanging drawer, keyboard tray and bottom shelf on the bench; a top shelf with dividers, a lower storage shelf, horizontal power rail and monitor arm on the uprighter.

Option 2 (selected): Flexi Workstation

During a visit to our manufacturing unit, we showed the client the Flexi Workstation and its range of layouts (in-line, back-to-back, U, X, L and T). They chose it. The configuration:

  • Postform laminated worktop for the scale, thermal printer and barcode scanner
  • Two tray assemblies for scissors, cutters and tape dispensers
  • A utility bin for printer rolls, tape and label sheets
  • An adjustable shelf for cartons and pouches
  • A vertical power rail for all devices
  • Monitor arm and keyboard tray for PC use
  • Overhead light for reading small labels

Weigh, print, bill, pack

How the setup works together

The scale, printer and scanner sit on the worktop in order of use. Consumables sit in the trays and utility bin, cartons on the shelf above, and the monitor floats on its arm, so the worktop stays free for the parcel.

Benefits for the client

What the configuration enabled

  • Workflow: tools, consumables and devices within reach, which shortens each order during peak periods
  • Adaptability: reconfigurable into in-line, U-shaped or back-to-back layouts, with starter and adder options for growth
  • Cost: a standardised system that meets current needs and allows upgrades without bespoke fabrication

Seeing the options helps

Furnisys perspective

The client started with one configuration and chose another after seeing both at our factory. Offering options from the same set of modules keeps that decision easy.

Frequently asked questions

What should an e-commerce packing station include?

A worktop for the scale, label printer and scanner, trays for cutters and tape, a bin for rolls and labels, shelves for cartons, power and good lighting.

Why choose a Flexi Workstation for dispatch?

It can be rearranged into in-line, back-to-back, U, X, L or T layouts and extended with adders as order volumes grow.

Can a monitor be mounted off the worktop?

Yes. A monitor arm and keyboard tray keep the PC setup ergonomic and free up the work surface.
