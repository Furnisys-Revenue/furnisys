Workspace glossary.

Plain-language definitions of the terms used in workbench, storage and workspace planning, from worksurface codes to lean methods.

#ABCDEFGHKLMOPRSUW

#

5S
A workplace organisation method: sort, set in order, shine, standardise and sustain. Pegboards, labelled bins and shadow-board drawers make 5S easier to keep up.

A

Adder
An add-on section that shares an upright with the section before it, used to extend a Link or Flexi run without repeating a full frame. See also Starter.
All Welded locker
A locker whose components are welded into one rigid unit and shipped fully assembled, suited to high-traffic, hard-use areas.

B

Back stop
A raised strip along the rear edge of a worksurface that stops parts and tools from rolling off the back.
Base cabinet
A floor-standing drawer or shutter cabinet that sits under or beside a workbench, often on a frame base to match bench height.
Base Series
Furnisys's all-purpose workbench series for general workspace needs, available in linear and L configurations.
Bin rail
A horizontal rail that holds hanging bins, keeping small parts visible and within reach above the worksurface.

C

Configurable
A buying route where a system is configured from pre-engineered modules for a critical application, without the risk of one-off fabrication.

D

Double-sided workstation
A workstation set up so operators can work from both sides, sharing one central frame and its accessories.

E

End stop
A raised strip on the side edge of a worksurface that keeps items from sliding off the end.
ESD
Electrostatic discharge. ESD-safe workstations use dissipative worksurfaces and grounding to protect sensitive electronic components.

F

FIFO
First in, first out. Flow racks load from the rear and pick from the front so the oldest stock is always used first.
Flexi System
Furnisys's most flexible work system, using a micro and macro slot frame to build workstations, universal carts and flow racks that share accessories.
Flow rack
A rack with inclined roller tracks that feeds bins or totes to the operator by gravity, used for picking, kitting and assembly.
Full depth cabinet
A Furnisys base or mobile cabinet with a 28.5 inch depth, giving more storage per drawer. See also Shallow depth cabinet.

G

GW
Worksurface code for GI sheet over plyboard, a durable top for maintenance and heavy-handling work.

H

Hanging drawer
A drawer unit mounted under the worksurface, adding storage without taking floor space.
HPL
High pressure laminate, a hard-wearing worksurface finish.

K

Kaizen
Continuous improvement. Modular systems support kaizen because modules can be added or moved as processes change.
Kitting
Gathering all the parts needed for a job into one kit before it reaches the workstation.
Knock Down locker
A locker assembled on site with rivets or nuts and bolts, so it ships flat and can be rearranged later.

L

Lean workspace
A workspace planned to remove non-value-adding activity (waste) so work flows on time with less cost and effort.
Levelling glides
Adjustable feet under legs and frames that level the structure on uneven floors.
Link System
Furnisys's system of uprighters, workstands, trolleys and flow racks. The Link uprighter can be mounted on Furnisys or existing workbenches.
Link uprighter
A vertical frame mounted on a workbench (or a wall) that carries shelves, panels, rails and lighting, turning a bench into a workstation.

M

Mobile cabinet
A drawer cabinet on heavy-duty castors, used where storage needs to move with the work.
Modular
Built from interchangeable components that can be combined, expanded or upgraded instead of replaced.

O

Outrigger
A frame foot that extends the base of a Flexi section for stability.

P

PELW
Worksurface code for postform electrostatic laminated particle board, an ESD-safe laminate top.
Pegboard
A perforated panel that holds hooks and holders so tools are stored in view at the point of use. Part of the Wall System.
PLW
Worksurface code for postform laminated particle board, a laminate top with a rounded front edge.
Point-of-use storage
Storing tools, parts and consumables exactly where the task is done, to cut walking and searching.
Powder coating
A baked-on paint finish that protects steel from corrosion and abrasion. Furnisys systems come in five standard RAL colours.
Power rail
A rail fitted with power sockets, bringing electrical supply to the work area.
Pre-configured
Proven setups that buyers have used repeatedly, packaged as ready-to-order products with part numbers.
Pro Series
Furnisys's industrial-grade workbench series with heavy-duty welded legs for rough, heavy-duty environments.

R

RAL
A European colour-matching standard used to specify powder coat colours, for example RAL 5010 Gentian Blue.
Riser shelf
A shelf raised above the worksurface to hold instruments or parts, freeing the work area.

S

Scitec Series
Furnisys's technical workbench series with fully enclosed legs, suited to labs, testing and electronics.
Shallow depth cabinet
A Furnisys base or mobile cabinet with a 22.5 inch depth, for tighter spaces. See also Full depth cabinet.
SSW
Worksurface code for stainless steel sheet over plyboard, a hygienic, chemical-resistant top.
Starter
The first section of a Link or Flexi run, with its own frame on both sides. Adders extend it.
Stringer
A horizontal member between workbench legs that adds rigidity and can act as a footrest.

U

Universal cart
A Flexi mobile cart that accepts the same shelves, panels, bins and rails as Flexi workstations.

W

Worksurface
The top of a workbench. Furnisys offers PLW, PELW, HPL, GW, SSW and other options to suit the task.
Workstand
A self-standing Link frame with its own worksurface, for light-duty operations.
