The Furnisys Podcast

Practical know-how for industrial workspaces, in short audio episodes you can play on the shop floor, on the road or between meetings. Learn how to set up, load and look after your benches, ESD stations, storage and flow racks.

Episode: 1

Setting up and levelling a new workbench

Most shop floors aren't flat. This episode walks through setting up a new bench in three steps: place it where the work happens, level it both ways on its glides, and run a quick pre-use check before anyone starts work.

Learn more about Furnisys workbenches, or watch the video version.

Loading a workbench right

Bench ratings assume a spread load. Learn the ratings of the bench and every accessory, how to spread heavy items or support them over a leg frame or cabinet, and why heavy items go low and light items high.

Learn more about the Base Series workbench, or watch the video version.

Episode: 3

Fitting Link Uprighter accessories

When everything ends up on the worktop, an uprighter frees the space. Understand starter and adder frames, plan accessories by reach, and fit each one into the same notch on both frames within its rating.

Learn more about Link system combinations, or watch the video version.

Episode: 4

Daily ESD checks at the bench

A two-minute routine for every shift: test your wrist strap, follow the grounding path from strap to mat to plug, and keep the mat clean and the work area clear.

Learn more about ESD workstations, or watch the video version.

Drawer partitions and 5S tool control

An open drawer hides problems. Sort the tools, set them in order with drawer partitions, label every compartment, then shine, standardise and sustain with a quick check each shift.

Loading flow racks for first in, first out

Static shelving works backwards. Load flow racks from the back and pick from the front, keep one part number per lane labelled at both ends, and stay within the rack and shelf ratings.
