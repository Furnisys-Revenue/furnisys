Getting started.

Everything you need from delivery to first use: get the site ready, install each product family correctly and know what the handover pack contains.

Site-readiness checklistInstallation by product familyHandover pack

Site-readiness checklist.

Tick these off before your installation date. A ready site means the team can install in one visit.

Floor

  • Firm, finished and dry where the furniture will stand
  • Uneven or trimix floors flagged in advance, so benches can be supplied with threaded stud levellers (M12 x 25, 10–15 mm adjustment)

Space and layout

  • Benches clear of walkways and door swings
  • Room behind each bench to reach power points and move chairs or trolleys
  • Heavy machines and precision instruments, such as balances, planned on separate benches

Power and earthing

  • Power points ready where power rails and outlet strips will connect
  • A qualified electrician available to connect rails and strips to a suitable supply
  • An earth point near every ESD bench

Access and handover

  • A clear route from the unloading point to the installation area
  • Someone on site who can check the installation and receive the handover pack

Installation by product family.

The key steps for each family. Our team installs your furniture; use these when you move, re-level or adjust it later.

Workbenches: Base, Scitec and Pro

  1. Place the bench on a firm floor, away from walkways and door swings.
  2. Level it: put a spirit level on the worktop along its length, then across its depth, and turn the glide under the low corner until the bubble is centred.
  3. Press down on each corner. If the bench rocks, adjust the glide under the corner that lifts.
  4. Set the working height: about 750–850 mm for seated work and 900–1100 mm for standing work. Fit leg extenders to every leg at the same height, then re-level.
  5. Keep the support rails and extra leg in place on benches 84" and wider.
Full instructions in the user manual

Link Uprighter and accessories

  1. Hook each accessory into the notches at the same height on both uprights.
  2. Press down until it seats fully.
  3. Check that it sits level before loading it.
  4. Have power rails and lights connected by a qualified electrician.
Full instructions in the user manual

Flexi System

  1. Set each worktop in the macro slots at the same height on every column (steps of 3").
  2. Use two people to lift a worktop and its brackets in or out of the slots.
  3. Check the worktop is level before loading.
  4. Contact us before adding stations or changing the layout, so we can supply the right adder sections, outriggers and worktops.
Full instructions in the user manual

Base and mobile cabinets

  1. Move a mobile cabinet with drawers closed and locked, pushing on the side handle, then lock the castors at the work point.
  2. Move a base cabinet on a forklift base with the top drawers emptied first; re-level a frame-base cabinet after moving it.
  3. Do not move base cabinets that support a Scitec or Pro worktop on their own. Contact us to relocate the whole bench.
Full instructions in the user manual

ESD kit

  1. Lay the ESD mat flat on the worktop, or use the PELW worktop directly.
  2. Snap a grounding cord onto the mat's press stud and connect it to the 3-way connector.
  3. Connect the grounding plug to your facility's earth point.
  4. Connect the wrist-strap cord to the 3-way connector as well.
Full instructions in the user manual

Check before first use

  • All legs, frames and uprights are firmly in place and nothing moves when you push the worktop
  • Castors on trolleys and mobile cabinets lock and unlock
  • Power rails and outlet strips are connected by a qualified electrician
  • Every bench is level and does not rock
  • Shelves and accessories are fully seated in their slots
